title: FAQ
description: Common questions about using Greenloop.
---

## Who can create users?

Only the **Org Admin** — no other role can, not even an Admin. Users are managed
in the separate **Accesswise** application. See
[User Management](/docs/user-management).

## Is billing based on categories or subcategories?

Always **subcategories**, never main categories. See
[Subcategories](/docs/catalog/subcategories).

## Do drivers use the web system?

No. Drivers use a separate **Android mobile app**. See the
[Driver Mobile Application](/docs/operations/routes/driver-app).

## What is the difference between a branch and a facility?

A **branch** is a demographic grouping (e.g. a state); a **facility** is a
physical warehouse under a branch. See [Facilities](/docs/system-resources/facilities).

## What happens when I save a work order?

A **pickup activity** for the driver and a **BOL** are created automatically. See
[Work Order Flow](/docs/operations/work-orders/work-order-flow).

## How are invoices generated?

Through **QuickBooks**, from the BOL. See
[Generating an Invoice](/docs/getting-started/generating-invoice).

## When does a pricing change need approval?

When a general price tier has **Need approval on customer linking** ticked, or
when the change is made by a role or person listed in
[Approval Rules](/docs/system-resources/configuration/approval-rules). Requests
appear in the approver's **My inbox**. See
[Approvals](/docs/customer-management/approvals).

## What is customer consent?

When **Require customer consent** is on for a customer, an approved quote must
be sent to the customer and accepted before it becomes their price tier. See
[Pricing & Quotes](/docs/customer-management/pricing-and-quotes#require-customer-consent).

## What is the difference between Status and Tags?

**Status** is the lifecycle stage (Lead, Prospect, Customer, Inactive,
Archived). **Tags** are configurable labels. See
[Tags](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#tags) and
[Lifecycle Transitions](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#lifecycle-transitions).

## How do I record items a customer drops off without a work order?

Create a [Walk-in BOL](/docs/operations/bols/walk-in-bols) from the Dashboard.

## How do I remove a duplicate customer?

Merge it into the correct profile with [Merge Jobs](/docs/customer-management/merge-jobs).
