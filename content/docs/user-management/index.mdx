User Management controls who can use Greenloop and what they can do. Access is
based on each user's **roles** and **facilities**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Users are created and managed in a separate application called **Accesswise**.
  Only the **Org Admin** can create users. **No other role can, not even an
  Admin.**
</Callout>

## Open Access Management

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open the account menu
    In Greenloop, click your **avatar** (top right).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Click Access Management
    Under **Account Settings**, click **Access Management**. Accesswise opens on
    your organization, with the tabs **Overview**, **Users**, **Branches**,
    **Facilities**, and **Subscriptions**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/account-menu.jpg" alt="Account menu" caption="Account Settings → Access Management" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## User listing

The **Users** tab shows every user with their **Name**, **Email**, **Reporting
Manager**, **Phone No**, **Mobile No**, and **Status**. Each user has options to
**deactivate**, **edit**, **give roles**, **make org admin**, **send password
reset email**, **set manager**, and **delete**. You can **Search name** and
**Filter by role**.
