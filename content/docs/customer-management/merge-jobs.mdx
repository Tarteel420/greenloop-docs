---
title: Merge Jobs
description: Combine duplicate customer profiles into one.
---

Merge Jobs let you combine customer profiles, which is useful for duplicates.
You choose a **source** profile and a **target** profile, then select what to
merge from the source into the target.

<UIPath items={["Customer Management", "Merge Jobs"]} />

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/merge-jobs-list.jpg" alt="Merge Jobs" caption="① Merge Job ② Active · Completed · Failed ③ Refresh" />

Merge jobs are listed under **Active**, **Completed**, and **Failed**, with
**Source**, **Target**, **Status**, **Created By**, and **Created At**. Click
**Refresh** to update the list.

## Merge two customers

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click Merge Job
    On the **Merge Jobs** page, click **+ Merge Job**. The **Merge Customer**
    window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the source and target
    In **Merge From (Source)**, select the duplicate profile. In **Merge To
    (Target)**, select the profile to keep.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/merge-modal.jpg" alt="Merge Customer" caption="① Merge From (Source) ② Merge To (Target) ③ Optional data to merge ④ Always transferred" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the data to merge
    Under **Data to Merge**, tick what to move from the source to the target:

    | Option | Contents |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Activities** | All activity, calls, emails, and meeting records |
    | **Notes** | All notes from the customer view |
    | **Contacts** | All contact persons with their details and roles |
    | **Addresses** | Billing, shipping, and business addresses |

    These are **always** transferred: **Work Orders**, **BOLs**, **Projects**,
    **Sub Customers**, **Emails**, and **Attachments**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Confirm
    Review the **Action Confirmation**: *Source customer's profile will be
    deleted after a successful merge operation.* The **Delete source customer
    profile** box is ticked by default. Click **Confirm Merge**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/merge-confirm.jpg" alt="Action Confirmation" caption="① Action Confirmation ② Confirm Merge" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Track the job
    The job appears under **Active**, then moves to **Completed** or **Failed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  With **Delete source customer profile** ticked, the **source customer is
  deleted** after the merge completes. Check that the source and target are
  correct before you click **Confirm Merge**.
</Callout>
