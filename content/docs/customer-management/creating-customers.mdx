---
title: Creating Customers
description: Add commercial and residential customers step by step.
---

The customer module holds both **commercial** (business) and **residential**
(individual) customers. The two forms collect different information.

<UIPath items={["Customer Management", "Customers", "New customer"]} />

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click New customer
    On the **Customers** page, click **New customer** and choose **Commercial**
    or **Residential**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/new-customer-dropdown.jpg" alt="New customer menu" caption="① Commercial / Residential" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tabs items={["Commercial customer", "Residential customer"]}>
<Tab value="Commercial customer">

The **Create a new commercial customer** page has four sections: **Primary
information**, **Addresses**, **Profile information**, and **Contacts**.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Primary information
    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/commercial-primary.jpg" alt="Primary information" caption="① Customer name ② Sales rep ③ Status and Lead source ④ Toggles ⑤ Copy address buttons" />

    | Field | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Customer name** * | Required. |
    | **Parent company** | The parent customer, if any. |
    | **Type** * | *Commercial* (pre-selected). |
    | **Status** | Lifecycle stage. New customers created here are set to **Customer**. |
    | **Lead source** | For example *Cold call*, *Customer called in*, *Referral*. |
    | **Competitor** · **Doing Business As (DBA)** | Free text. |
    | **Business unit** | From [Business Units](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#business-units). |
    | **Sales rep** | Opens the **Add Sales Rep** window (see below). |
    | **EPA #** · **Website** | EPA number and website. |
    | Toggles | **Send statement**, **Cooperate customer**, **Opportunity for old customer**, **Send COR with invoice** (Certificate of Recycling), **R2 Certified**, **Bill root parent**, **Create as vendor**. |
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add the sales rep(s)
    Click the **Sales rep** field. In **Add Sales Rep**, choose the **Sales rep**,
    their **Percentage** share, and **Region**. Click **+ Add Sales Rep** to split
    the customer between several reps, then click **Add**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/add-sales-rep.jpg" alt="Add Sales Rep" caption="① Sales rep · Percentage · Region ② Add another rep ③ Add" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Addresses
    Enter the **Shipping** and **Billing** addresses (Address Line 1–2, City,
    State, Zip Code, Country). Use **Copy Billing address** / **Copy Shipping
    address** to copy one into the other.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Profile information
    Set the operational and billing rules for the customer.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/commercial-profile.jpg" alt="Profile information" caption="① Require customer consent ② Requires P.O # ③ Terms, hours, branches, surcharges, guaranteed destruction, tags" />

    | Field | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Require customer consent** | *Pre-filled from your organization default. You can change it for this customer now or later from the customer page.* See [Pricing & Quotes](/docs/customer-management/pricing-and-quotes). |
    | **Requires P.O #** · **P.O #** · **PO Expiry** | Purchase order rules. |
    | **Payment Method** · **Payment Term** | Billing terms. |
    | **Feet / Inches (Clearance Height)** | Clearance height at the customer's site. |
    | **Starting hours** · **Ending hours** | Operating hours. |
    | **Branches** | The branches that serve this customer. |
    | **Additional surcharges** | From [Additional Surcharge](/docs/pricing-billing/surcharges). |
    | **Guaranteed destruction** | **Hard Drives Only**, **All E-Waste**, **Asset Log on Hard Drives Only**, **Asset Log on All E-Waste Items**. |
    | **Tags** | Labels from [Tags](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#tags). |
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Contacts
    Click **Add Contact**. Choose the **Contact type** (**Primary**, **Account
    Payable**, or **Secondary**), enter the **First name** (required), last name,
    title, email, mobile, and **Phone #** (required), then click **Add**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/add-contact.jpg" alt="Add Contact details" caption="① Contact type ② Name ③ Mobile / Phone # ④ Add" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Save** at the bottom of the page. The customer is created and you can
    open it from the Customers list.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/commercial-contacts.jpg" alt="Contacts section and Save" caption="① Add Contact ② Save" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Tab>
<Tab value="Residential customer">

The **Create a new residential customer** page is shorter.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/residential-form.jpg" alt="Create a new residential customer" caption="① Customer name ② Status ③ Sales rep ④ Branches" />

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### General details
    Enter the **Customer name** (required), choose the **Status** (required),
    and optionally add the **Sales rep** and **Branches**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Address details
    Enter **Address line 1**, **City**, and **Country** (required), plus
    Address line 2, State, and Zip code.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="info" title="Status vs Tags">
  **Status** (Lead, Prospect, Customer, Inactive, Archived) is the lifecycle
  stage. **Tags** are configurable labels. To create a record at the Prospect
  stage, use [Prospects → New prospect](/docs/customer-management/prospects).
  Leads are created in [Lead Management](/docs/lead-management/leads).
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Which fields are required depends on
  [Required/Optional Fields](/docs/system-resources/configuration/required-optional-fields).
</Callout>
