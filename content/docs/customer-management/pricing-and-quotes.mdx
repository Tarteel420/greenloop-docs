Each customer's pricing is managed at the bottom of the customer's **Details**
tab. From here you create **price tier quotes** and **project quotes**, send
them for customer consent, and email the current pricing to the customer.

## Require customer consent

The **Require customer consent** toggle is pre-filled from the **Organization
default** (see [Pricing & Quotes configuration](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#pricing--quotes)).
The app explains it as follows:

> When on, an approved quote for this customer must be sent to them and accepted
> before it becomes their price tier. When off, internal approval is the last
> step and the quote is scheduled straight away. Each quote remembers the
> setting it was created under, so changing this never affects a quote already
> in progress.

Internal approval still applies either way. A quote that needs approval under
your [approval rules](/docs/system-resources/configuration/approval-rules) goes
for approval first, whether or not customer consent is required.

### When a customer differs from the organization default

If you change the toggle so it no longer matches the organization default, the
card shows a **Custom** badge and the line *Your organization default is On*
(or *Off*). Click **Reset to org default** to go back to the organization
setting.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/customer-consent-custom.jpg" alt="Custom consent setting" caption="① Custom badge ② Organization default and Reset to org default ③ Toggle" />

## Pricing cards

The **Current** tab shows a card for each price tier and project quote. The
badge shows its state, for example **Current Tier** (*Currently in force for
this customer*) or **Requires Customer Consent** (*Project in progress for this
customer*). **History** lists earlier tiers and quotes.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/customer-pricing-cards.jpg" alt="Pricing cards" caption="① Send pricing ② Send for Customer Consent ③ History of this tier" />

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Send pricing** | *Email the current pricing to the customer. No approval link, no status change.* Opens the pricing template preview (see [below](#send-pricing)). |
| **Send for Customer Consent** | Sends a quote that is waiting for consent to the customer. |
| History icon | Shows the history of that tier or quote. |

Project quote cards also show the **Scope** (for example *All branches (flat
price)* or *Fox Branch (specific branch)*), the **Project Period**, and how many
items are overridden (for example **1 ITEM OVERRIDE →**).

## Create a price tier quote

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click Create Price Tier Quote
    In the **Pricing** section, click **+ Create Price Tier Quote**. The **Amend
    Price Tier Quote** window opens for the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the type and tier
    Choose the **Type**:

    - **General**: pick an existing general price tier in **Name**.
    - **Customer Specific**: pick a customer-specific tier, or click **+ New
      customer specific/national contract** to create one. When the customer
      already has an active tier, you can copy its prices and raise or lower
      them by a percentage or dollar amount. See
      [Adjust copied prices](/docs/pricing-billing/customer-specific-price-tiers#adjust-copied-prices).

    <Carousel images={[
      {"src": "/images/customer-management/price-tier-quote-general.jpg", "alt": "Amend Price Tier Quote, General", "caption": "General: ① Type ② Name ③ Effective date ④ Effective immediately after customer consent ⑤ Expiry date ⑥ Save"},
      {"src": "/images/customer-management/price-tier-quote-cs.jpg", "alt": "Amend Price Tier Quote, Customer Specific", "caption": "Customer Specific: ① Type ② New customer specific/national contract"},
    ]} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set the dates
    Enter the **Effective date** first, then the **Expiry date** (both
    required), or tick **Effective immediately after customer consent**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Save**. Depending on your [approval rules](/docs/system-resources/configuration/approval-rules),
    the quote may need internal approval (see [Approvals](/docs/customer-management/approvals)).
    - If **Require customer consent** is **off**, the new tier is scheduled
      straight away (no email to the customer).
    - If it's **on**, click **Send for Customer Consent** on the new card to
      email it to the customer. It becomes their price tier once accepted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create a project quote

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click New Project Quote
    In the **Pricing** section, click **+ New Project Quote**. The **Create new
    project** page opens with the **Customer** pre-filled.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/project-quote-form.jpg" alt="Create new project" caption="① Customer (pre-filled) ② Project name ③ Price mode ④ Start / End date" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Fill in the project
    | Field | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Project name** * | Required. |
    | **Price mode** * | **National Contract** or **Branch Based**. |
    | **Opportunity** | Link the project to an [opportunity](/docs/customer-management/opportunities). |
    | **Start date** · **End date** | The project period. |
    | **Description** · **Notes** | Free text. |
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Save the project. It appears as a card in the **Pricing** section and under
    [Projects](/docs/customer-management/projects).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send pricing

Click **Send pricing** on a price tier card to open the **Pricing Template**
page. It shows the customer's prices per branch, laid out using the
[Basic Template](/docs/pricing-billing/basic-template).

<Screenshot src="/images/pricing-billing/send-pricing-preview.jpg" alt="Pricing template preview" caption="① Back ② Edit Template ③ Send ④ Prices per branch" />

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Review the prices
    Check the prices shown for each branch.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Edit the template (optional)
    Click **Edit Template** to change the layout for this customer. Use **Add
    Item** to add categories or items and **Notes** to add notes. Click **Update
    Template** to keep your changes, **Restore to Basic Template** to start over,
    or **Cancel**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/pricing-billing/send-pricing-edit.jpg" alt="Editing the pricing template" caption="① Restore to Basic Template ② Update Template ③ Add Item ④ Notes" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Send
    Click **Send** to email the pricing to the customer. Sent emails appear in
    the customer's [Emails tab](/docs/customer-management/customer-details#emails).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Price tiers themselves are created under
  [Pricing & Billing](/docs/pricing-billing). Branch Threshold Pricing is also
  listed there, but it isn't covered in this guide.
</Callout>
