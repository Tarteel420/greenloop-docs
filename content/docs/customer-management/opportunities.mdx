An **opportunity** is a potential deal with a customer. It records the projected
value, the stage, and the expected close date so sales can track the pipeline.

<UIPath items={["Customer Management", "Opportunities"]} />

The page has three views: **List**, **Pipeline**, and **Closed**.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/opportunities-list.jpg" alt="Opportunities" caption="① List / Pipeline / Closed ② Date ③ Reset ④ Stage cards ⑤ New opportunity" />

## Date filter and stage cards

- **Date**: choose the **Date field** (**Expected close** or **Created**) and a
  **Range** (**This month**, **Next 30 days**, **This quarter**, **Next
  quarter**, **This year**, **All time**, or **Custom range…**). Click **Reset**
  to go back to the default.
- **Stage cards**: one card per open stage (**Open**, **In Discussion**,
  **Issued Estimates**, **Purchasing**) plus **All stages**. Each card shows the
  **weighted** total in large type, the stage weight (for example *80% weight*),
  the projected total (**proj**), and the number of customers.

<Callout type="info" title="Weighted vs projected">
  The **projected** amount is the value entered on the opportunity. The
  **weighted** amount is the projected amount × the stage probability. For
  example, $5,000.50 in **Purchasing** (80%) is $4,000.40 weighted. Stage
  probabilities are set in
  [Opportunity Statuses](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#opportunity-statuses).
</Callout>

## List view

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/opportunities-table.jpg" alt="Opportunities list" caption="① Search ② Stage ③ All / Overdue / Stale ④ Group by ⑤ Overdue badge ⑥ Expand row" />

| Tool | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Search ID, title, customer** | Find an opportunity. |
| Stage menu (**All stages**) | Show one stage only. |
| **All** / **Overdue** / **Stale** | **Overdue** shows open opportunities past their expected close date. **Stale** shows opportunities flagged as stale. |
| **Group by** | **None**, **Customer**, **Sales rep**, or **Stage**. |

The list shows the **Opportunity** (number, title, and customer), **Sales rep**,
**Stage**, **Expected close** (with an **Overdue** badge when late), **Last
activity**, **Projected**, and **Weighted**.

### Group the list

Choose a **Group by** option to collapse the list into groups. Each group row
shows the count (for example *1 opportunity · 1 overdue*) and the projected and
weighted totals. Click **›** to expand a group.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/opportunities-grouped.jpg" alt="Grouped by customer" caption="① Group by Customer ② Expanded group ③ Collapsed group" />

### Expand a row

Click **+** on a row to see **Reports to**, **Probability**, and **Days in
stage**, and these actions:

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/opportunity-row-expanded.jpg" alt="Expanded opportunity" caption="① Details ② Edit · Mark as won · Mark as lost · Flag as stale ③ Open full record" />

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Opens the **Edit opportunity** panel (see [below](#edit-an-opportunity)). |
| **Mark as won** | Moves the opportunity to **Closed Won**. |
| **Mark as lost** | Moves the opportunity to **Closed Lost**. |
| **Flag as stale** | Flags it so a manager can review it. Flagged opportunities appear under **Stale**. |
| **Open full record** | Opens the opportunity's own page. |

### Edit an opportunity

**Edit** opens a panel on the right, so you don't leave the list. *Changes show
up in the pipeline as soon as you save.*

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/opportunity-edit-drawer.jpg" alt="Edit opportunity" caption="① Stage ② Projected amount · Expected close ③ Weighted value ④ Save changes" />

You can change the **Title**, **Customer**, **Stage**, **Projected amount**,
**Expected close**, **Lead source**, and **Description**. The **Weighted value**
updates from the projected amount and the stage weight. Click **Save changes**.

### Closing checks

Greenloop checks the opportunity before it lets you close it. For example, an
opportunity can't be marked as lost while work is linked to it. The message
explains why and how many BOLs or projects are linked.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/opportunity-lost-blocked.jpg" alt="Can't be marked as lost" caption="① Reason the opportunity can't be closed" />

<Callout type="warn">
  To close an opportunity that is no longer being pursued, first unlink the BOL
  or project from it.
</Callout>

## Pipeline view

The **Pipeline** view shows open opportunities per sales rep and stage.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/opportunities-pipeline.jpg" alt="Pipeline view" caption="① Lead with: Weighted / Projected ② Stage columns" />

- **Lead with**: show the amounts as **Weighted** (default) or **Projected**.
- Each row is a sales rep. Each cell shows the amount and the number of
  customers (**cust**) for that stage. **Total** adds up all stages.
- Managers appear with a **Team** badge (for example *Team · 3*). The table ends
  with **No sales rep** and a **Grand total**.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/opportunities-pipeline-rows.jpg" alt="Pipeline rows" caption="① A rep's amount for one stage ② Team rows" />

### See a team

Click a team row to drill into that manager's team. You see the manager's **own
deals**, each team member, and **Total – [name]'s team**. The stage cards
update to that team. Click **Everyone** or **← Back to Everyone's team** to go
back.

<Carousel images={[
  {"src": "/images/customer-management/opportunities-pipeline-team.jpg", "alt": "Team breadcrumb", "caption": "① Everyone / team breadcrumb ② Back to Everyone's team"},
  {"src": "/images/customer-management/opportunities-pipeline-team-rows.jpg", "alt": "Team rows", "caption": "① Manager's own deals ② Team total"},
]} />

<Callout type="info">
  Each customer is counted once in a total, even if more than one rep works with
  them. That's why the customer count can be lower than the rows above added
  together.
</Callout>

### See what's behind a number

Click an amount in the table. A panel opens for that rep and stage.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/opportunities-pipeline-drawer.jpg" alt="Pipeline cell panel" caption="① Weighted, projected, opportunities, customers ② Opportunities / Customers tabs ③ All / Overdue / Stale ④ Sort ⑤ Open in list view" />

- The top shows the **Weighted** and **Projected** totals and the number of
  **Opportunities** and **Customers**.
- **Opportunities** lists each deal with its customer, location, expected close,
  days in stage, and last activity.
- **Customers** lists each customer once, with its totals. Click **›** to expand.
- Filter with **All**, **Overdue**, and **Stale**, and sort by **Expected close
  (soonest)**, **Projected (highest)**, **Days in stage (longest)**, or **Last
  activity (oldest)**.
- Use **⋮** on an opportunity for **Open opportunity**, **Edit opportunity**,
  **Mark as won**, **Mark as lost**, and **Flag as stale**.
- Click **Open in list view** to see the same opportunities in the List view.

## Closed view

The **Closed** view lists **Closed Won** and **Closed Lost** opportunities.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/opportunities-closed.jpg" alt="Closed view" caption="① Closed ② Closed Won / Closed Lost totals ③ All / Won / Lost" />

The two cards show the count and total for each outcome. Filter with **All**,
**Won**, or **Lost**, or search by ID, title, or customer. The list shows the
**Opportunity**, **Sales rep**, **Outcome**, **Closed** date, and **Amount**.

## Create an opportunity

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click New opportunity
    On the **Opportunities** page, click **+ New opportunity**. The **New
    opportunity** panel opens on the right: *It shows up in the pipeline as
    soon as you create it.*

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/opportunity-form.jpg" alt="New opportunity" caption="① Title ② Customer ③ Sales rep · Stage ④ Projected amount · Expected close ⑤ Lead source ⑥ Create opportunity" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Fill in the details
    | Field | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Title** * | A short name for the deal. |
    | **Customer** * | The customer the deal is for. |
    | **Sales rep** * | Pick a customer first; then choose the rep. |
    | **Stage** * | Starts at **Open** (10%). |
    | **Projected amount** * | The full value of the deal. |
    | **Expected close** * | When you expect to close the deal. |
    | **Lead source** * | Where the deal came from. |
    | **Description** | Optional. |

    The **Weighted value** (projected × stage weight) is calculated for you.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Create
    Click **Create opportunity**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Opportunity statuses are configured in
  [General → Opportunity Statuses](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#opportunity-statuses).
  A [project quote](/docs/customer-management/pricing-and-quotes#create-a-project-quote)
  can be linked to an opportunity. A customer's own opportunities are also on
  its [Opportunities tab](/docs/customer-management/customer-details#opportunities).
</Callout>
