The **Customers** page lists every customer (and, on its **Prospects** tab,
every prospect) with their shipping and billing
addresses, **Tags**, **Status**, **Sales Rep**, **Hierarchy**, **Business
Unit**, **Corporate**, **Type**, and **Date Created**. Scroll right to see all
columns.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/customers-list.jpg" alt="Customers list" caption="① New customer ② Search ③ Columns · Advance Filters · Filters · View settings ④ Customers / Prospects tabs" />

## Find a customer

<Tabs items={["Search", "Filters", "Advance Filters", "View settings"]}>
  <Tab value="Search">
    Type in **Search customers…** to find a customer by name or address. The
    search runs across **both** the **Customers** and **Prospects** tabs, so you
    don't have to check two lists. See [Search customers and prospects together](#search-customers-and-prospects-together).
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Filters">
    Click **Filters** to open the filter panel: **Sales person**, **Type**,
    **Business Unit**, **Lead Source**, **Customer Tags**, and **Price tier**.
    Click the refresh icon to reset, or **✕** to close.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/customers-filters.jpg" alt="Customer filters" caption="Filters panel" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Advance Filters">
    Click **Advance Filters** to build a rule: choose a **Column**, an
    **Operator** (for example *Equals*), and a **Value**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/customers-advance-filters.jpg" alt="Advance Filters" caption="① Column · Operator · Value" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="View settings">
    Click the **gear** icon to choose which records to show: **Active**,
    **Inactive**, and/or **Archived**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Search customers and prospects together

The page has two tabs: **Customers** and **Prospects**. Each tab shows how many
records it holds. When you type in the search box, both counts update to show
how many matches each tab has. A **Search** chip appears under the tabs, with
**Clear** to remove it.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/customers-search-tabs.jpg" alt="Search counts on the Customers and Prospects tabs" caption="① Search ② Customers / Prospects tabs with match counts ③ Search chip · Clear" />

If there are no matching customers but there are matching prospects, open the
**Prospects** tab to see them. The search stays applied when you switch tabs.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/customers-prospects-tab.jpg" alt="Matches on the Prospects tab" caption="① Same search ② Prospects tab with 3 matches ③ New prospect" />

<Callout type="info">
  Prospects no longer have their own menu item. Open them from the **Prospects**
  tab on this page. See [Prospects](/docs/customer-management/prospects).
  In the sprint demo, the team said that when one tab has no matches the page
  switches to the tab that does. In our QA check the page stayed on the
  **Customers** tab (showing 0) and we opened **Prospects** ourselves.
</Callout>

## Quick view

Hover over a customer's name to see a quick view with the **Status**,
addresses, contacts, and profile details (payment terms, payment method, sales
reps, branches). Click **View Details** to open the full
[customer page](/docs/customer-management/customer-details).

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/customer-quick-view.jpg" alt="Customer quick view" caption="① View Details ② Hover over the customer name" />

<Callout type="info">
  Clicking a customer name opens the customer page in a **new tab**.
</Callout>

## Bulk actions

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Select customers
    Tick the checkbox next to one or more customers. A bar shows how many are
    selected, with **Select all … customers** to select every customer.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose an action
    Click an icon on the right of the bar:
    - **Set customer sales rep**
    - **Set customer status**
  </Step>
</Steps>
