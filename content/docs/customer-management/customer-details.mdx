Open a customer from the **Customers** list (or **View Details** in the quick
view). Everything about the customer is on this page, organized in tabs.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/customer-detail-header.jpg" alt="Customer page header" caption="① Tabs (use › to scroll to more tabs) ② Make Inactive ③ Edit tags ④ Edit general information" />

## Header actions

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Tags** ✏️ | Add or remove the customer's tags. |
| **Make Inactive** | Moves the customer to the **Inactive** status. Who can do this is set in [Lifecycle Transitions](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#lifecycle-transitions). |

## Details

The **Details** tab shows **General information**, **Addresses**, **Profile
Information**, and **Contacts**. Click the **pencil** icon on a card to edit it.
In **Contacts**, click **+ New contact** to add a contact, or the pencil / bin
icons to edit or delete one.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/customer-detail-contacts.jpg" alt="Contacts card" caption="① New contact ② Edit contact" />

Below the contacts are the **Require customer consent** setting and the
**Pricing** section (price tiers and project quotes). See
[Pricing & Quotes](/docs/customer-management/pricing-and-quotes).

## Activities

Tabs **To Do**, **Completed**, **Cancelled**, and **All**, with filters for
**Assigned user**, **Type**, search, and **Date range**. Click **New activity**
to schedule one for this customer. → [Activities](/docs/customer-management/activities)

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-activities.jpg" alt="Activities tab" caption="① Status tabs ② New activity" />

## Addresses

A customer can have **multiple locations**, each with its own address and
contact. Choose the **Address Type** (for example *Shipping Address*) to switch
lists. The default address is marked **Default**. Click **New address** to add
one, or use **⋮** on a row.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-addresses.jpg" alt="Addresses tab" caption="① Address Type ② New address ③ Default address" />

## Notes

Operational instructions for the customer. Click **New note** to add one. Notes
show who created them and when. They can be pinned and tagged (for example
**Pickup**). Use **⋮** on a note for its actions. Switch between list and grid
views with the icons next to **New note**.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-notes.jpg" alt="Notes tab" caption="① New note ② Pinned note with tag" />

## Sub Customers

Customers grouped under this customer, with the same search, filter, and column
tools as the main list. Click **New customer** to add one from here.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-sub-customers.jpg" alt="Sub Customers tab" caption="① New customer" />

## Emails

Emails sent to the customer (for example invoices and price tiers), with
**Subject**, **From**, **To & Delivery Status** (for example *Accepted*),
**Message Date**, and **Engagement**. Filter by **Status**.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-emails.jpg" alt="Emails tab" caption="① Status filter ② Delivery status" />

## Attachments

Files attached to the customer, with **Size**, **Uploaded at**, and **Uploaded
by**. Click **Upload** to add a file. Use **⋮** on a row for its actions.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-attachments.jpg" alt="Attachments tab" caption="① Upload ② Row menu" />

## Opportunities

This customer's opportunities (**Opportunity**, **Date**, **Sales Rep**,
**Projected Total**, **Status**, **Actual Total**). Click **New opportunity**.
→ [Opportunities](/docs/customer-management/opportunities)

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-opportunities.jpg" alt="Opportunities tab" caption="Opportunities tab" />

## Lifecycle

**Customer Lifecycle Journey**: every stage the record has been through and its
**Current Stage**. Transitions are recorded from the next stage change onwards.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-lifecycle.jpg" alt="Lifecycle tab" caption="① Current Stage" />

## Work Orders

**Pending** and **Completed** work orders for the customer, with **Columns** and
**Filters**, a **Search location…** box, and **New work order**. If any work
order has been rescheduled, a warning shows how many times, with **View
details**. → [Work Orders](/docs/operations/work-orders)

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-work-orders.jpg" alt="Work Orders tab" caption="① Pending / Completed ② New work order ③ Reschedule warning" />

## Projects

Projects for this customer under the **Pending**, **Active**, **Completed**, and
**Rejected** tabs. → [Projects](/docs/customer-management/projects)

## BOLs

The customer's BOLs, with the same search and filters as the
[BOLs page](/docs/operations/bols), plus **Send Bulk COR** (Certificate of
Recycling).

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-bols.jpg" alt="BOLs tab" caption="① Send Bulk COR" />

## Transactions

Invoices and payments from QuickBooks: **Date**, **Type**, **Number**,
**Amount**, **Sales Tax**, **Due Date**, **Balance Remaining**, **Terms**,
**P.O**, **Rep**, **Status**, and **Ship Via**. Filter by **Date Range** and
**Show**. Click **Refresh** to reload.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-transactions.jpg" alt="Transactions tab" caption="① Date Range · Show ② Refresh" />

## Line Items

The billing breakdown per invoice: **Invoice #**, **BOL #**, **Invoice Date**,
**Item Name**, **Item Desc**, **Quantity**, **Price**, and **Amount**.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-line-items.jpg" alt="Line Items tab" caption="① Refresh" />

## Sales Data

A **QuickBooksOnline** summary (for example last and first invoice dates, last
payment, average and lifetime sales amounts, terms, credit limit) and a
monthly sales table by year.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-sales-data.jpg" alt="Sales Data tab" caption="① QuickBooks summary ② Monthly sales" />

## QuickBooks Linked Profile

Compares the **Greenloop Customer Information** with the linked QuickBooks
profile. Use **Link Customer Profile from QuickBooks** to search for a
QuickBooks profile, or click **+ QuickBooks Profile**. Click **Unlink** to
remove the link.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/tab-quickbooks.jpg" alt="QuickBooks Linked Profile tab" caption="① QuickBooks Profile ② Unlink" />
