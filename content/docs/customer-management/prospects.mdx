**Prospects** are records at the **Prospect** lifecycle stage: potential
customers you are working to convert. They come from
[promoted leads](/docs/lead-management/promoting-leads) or are created directly
here.

<UIPath items={["Customer Management", "Customers", "Prospects tab"]} />

Prospects are on the **Prospects** tab of the
[Customers](/docs/customer-management/customers-list) page. The search box
searches customers and prospects together and shows the number of matches on
each tab.

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/customers-prospects-tab.jpg" alt="Prospects tab" caption="① Search ② Prospects tab ③ New prospect" />

The **Prospects** list works like the [Customers list](/docs/customer-management/customers-list):
**Search customers…**, **Columns**, **Advance Filters**, and **Filters**, with
the **Shipping Address** and **Billing Address** of each record. Click a name to
open its [details page](/docs/customer-management/customer-details).

## Create a prospect

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click New prospect
    On the **Prospects** tab, click **+ New prospect**. The **Create a new
    commercial customer** form opens with **Status** set to **Prospect**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/prospect-form.jpg" alt="New prospect form" caption="① Status = Prospect ② Lead source ③ Business unit" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Fill in the form
    Complete the same sections as a commercial customer: **Primary information**,
    **Addresses**, **Profile information**, and **Contacts**. Fields marked * are
    required. See [Creating Customers](/docs/customer-management/creating-customers)
    for each field.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  In the form above, **Lead source** and **Business unit** are marked as
  required. Required fields are set per status in
  [Required/Optional Fields](/docs/system-resources/configuration/required-optional-fields).
  When the prospect becomes a customer, change the **Status** (for example with
  **Set customer status** in the list's bulk actions). Who can move a record
  between stages is set in
  [Lifecycle Transitions](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#lifecycle-transitions).
</Callout>
