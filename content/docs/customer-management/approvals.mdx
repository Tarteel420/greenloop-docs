The **Approvals** module handles workflow-based decisions, for example a new
customer price tier or a new project quote that needs sign-off before it takes
effect. Who needs approval is set in
[Approval Rules](/docs/system-resources/configuration/approval-rules).

## Tabs

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/approvals-inbox.jpg" alt="Approvals: My inbox" caption="My inbox · Submitted by me · All approvals" />

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **My inbox** | Requests waiting on you, with **Customer**, **Type**, **Step**, **Role**, and **Status**. When empty: *Nothing is waiting on you.* |
| **Submitted by me** | Requests you submitted. |
| **All approvals** | Every request, with **Customer**, **Type**, **Status**, and **Submitted** date and time. |

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/approvals-all.jpg" alt="All approvals" caption="① Status (for example In Progress, Approved) ② Open" />

Examples of request **Type**: *New customer price tier*, *New project quote*.

## Open a request

Click **Open** on a row. The details page shows the request's version (for
example **Version 1**) and status (for example **In Progress** or **Approved**).

<Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/approval-detail.jpg" alt="Approval details" caption="① Summary ② Approval progress ③ Legend" />

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Summary** | **Type**, **Customer**, **Submitted by**, and the **Project** or **Pricing type** (for example *Branch Base*). |
| **Approval progress** | Each approver step, with the person, their role (for example *Sales Manager*), and the step's status. Steps are shown in order when several approvals are needed. |
| Pricing table | The items being changed, with **Category**, **Current**, and **Proposed** prices (and **Threshold** where it applies). |

Click the **ⓘ** icon to see the status legend: **Pending**, **Approved**,
**Rejected**, **Mixed**, **Not needed**, and **You** (your step).

When an approver has left a comment on their step, click **View comment** to
read it.

## What an approver does

Approvers review the request from **My inbox**, then approve or reject it and
can add comments for audit tracking.

<Callout type="info">
  The approve / reject controls only show for the approver of the current step.
  They were not visible to the account used to write this guide, so they are not
  shown here.
</Callout>

## After approval

If the customer has **Require customer consent** switched on, an approved quote
must then be sent to the customer and accepted before it becomes their price
tier. See [Pricing & Quotes](/docs/customer-management/pricing-and-quotes#require-customer-consent).
Linking a general price tier that has **Need approval on customer linking**
switched on also creates an approval request. → [General Price Tiers](/docs/pricing-billing/general-price-tiers)
