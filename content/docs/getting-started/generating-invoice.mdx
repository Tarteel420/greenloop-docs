---
title: Generating an Invoice
description: How a signed-off BOL becomes an invoice through QuickBooks.
---

Once items are processed, the recorded quantities and weights can be billed.
Invoices are created through **QuickBooks**.

<UIPath items={["Operations", "BOLs"]} />

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Find signed-off BOLs
    Open **Operations → BOLs**. Click **Stage**, tick **Signed off**, and click
    **Apply**. Only signed-off BOLs are shown. In the **Board** view, signed-off
    BOLs are in the **Signed off** column.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/stage-filter.jpg" alt="Stage filter" caption="The Stage filter lists every BOL stage." />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Open the BOL and review it
    Click the BOL number. In the **Details** tab, all three steps
    (**Pickup/drop-off information**, **Labels Information**, **Summary**) show a
    green tick, with **Signed off by** and **Date & time**. Click **Review
    steps** to go back through the processed items.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Click Generate invoice
    Click **Generate invoice**. Greenloop sends the invoice request to QuickBooks.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/generate-invoice.jpg" alt="Generate invoice" caption="① Status Signed off ② Generate invoice" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check the documents
    When the BOL is **Invoiced**, click **View BOL Documents** to see the
    invoice and other documents.
    → [BOL Documents](/docs/operations/bols/bol-documents)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Documents you can access

For invoiced BOLs, the documents can include:

- Invoice
- Bill of lading (BOL)
- Certificate of Recycling
- Certificate of Destruction

<Callout type="info">
  Pricing comes from the price tier linked to the customer, plus any
  surcharges. Billing is by [subcategory](/docs/catalog/subcategories). See
  [Pricing & Billing](/docs/pricing-billing).
</Callout>
