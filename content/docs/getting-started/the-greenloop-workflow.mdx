This page shows the full path of a job through Greenloop. Each stage links to
detailed instructions.

## End-to-end flow

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Lead → Prospect → Customer
    A lead is created or imported, then promoted to a prospect. Prospects become
    customers. You can also create a customer directly from **Customers → New customer**.
    → [Leads](/docs/lead-management/leads) · [Prospects](/docs/customer-management/prospects) · [Creating Customers](/docs/customer-management/creating-customers)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pricing agreed
    A price tier is linked to the customer through a **price tier quote** or a
    **project quote**. It may need internal approval and customer consent.
    → [Pricing & Quotes](/docs/customer-management/pricing-and-quotes)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Work order created
    The work order says what to collect, where, and when. Saving it creates a
    pickup activity for the driver and a BOL.
    → [Creating a Work Order](/docs/operations/work-orders/creating-a-work-order)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Route planned
    The work order goes on a route with a truck and a driver.
    → [Dispatch](/docs/operations/routes)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Driver pickup
    The driver collects the items, prints QR labels, and records pickup and
    drop-off details in the mobile app.
    → [Driver Mobile Application](/docs/operations/routes/driver-app)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Warehouse processing
    Items are received and, where the category requires it, go through triage
    and sorting & counting. Walk-in BOLs cover materials a customer drops off
    themselves. → [Processing Items Through the Warehouse](/docs/getting-started/warehouse-flow) · [Walk-in BOLs](/docs/operations/bols/walk-in-bols)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Sign-off and invoice
    Once all steps are complete and the BOL is **Signed off**, click
    **Generate invoice** to send it to QuickBooks.
    → [Generating an Invoice](/docs/getting-started/generating-invoice)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## BOL status progression

A BOL's status tracks where the job is:

**Waiting for pickup → Pickup completed → In processing → Ready for sign off → Signed off → Invoiced**

<Callout type="info">
  The processing path each material takes (Receiver → Triage → Sorting &
  Counting) is set per category in the [Catalog](/docs/catalog/categories).
</Callout>
