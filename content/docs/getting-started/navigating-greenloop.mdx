## Open the main menu

Click the **menu icon** (top left) to open the sidebar. Click a module to expand
its sub-menu, then click a page.

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/nav-sidebar.jpg" alt="Greenloop main menu" caption="① Menu icon. The sidebar lists every module you can access." />

| Module | Pages | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Home screen with widgets | [Dashboard](/docs/getting-started/dashboard) |
| **Customer Management** | Customers (with a Prospects tab), Activities, Projects, Opportunities, Approvals, Merge Jobs | [Customer Management](/docs/customer-management) |
| **Lead Management** | Overview, Leads, Activities | [Lead Management](/docs/lead-management) |
| **Operations** | Overview, Work orders, BOLs, Dispatch, Fleet | [Operations](/docs/operations) |
| **Pricing & Billing** | Price tiers (General, Customer specific), Price adjustments, Branch Threshold Pricing, Additional Surcharge, Templates | [Pricing & Billing](/docs/pricing-billing) |
| **Catalog** | Categories, Assets, Packaging, Essentials | [Catalog](/docs/catalog) |
| **Reports** | Lead Conversions By Sales Rep | [Reports](/docs/reports) |
| **Configuration** | Required/Optional Fields, General, Approval Rules, BOL stage limits, Email Templates | [Configuration](/docs/system-resources/configuration) |

<Callout type="info">
  Which modules you see depends on your role. If a page shows **No permission**,
  your role doesn't have access to it. Contact your administrator.
</Callout>

## The account menu

Click your **avatar** (top right) to see your name, role, and email, plus
**Account Settings**: **My Account**, **Preferences**, **Organization**,
**Access Management**, and **Logout**.

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/account-menu.jpg" alt="Account menu" caption="① Avatar. Account Settings links to your profile (My Account) and to Access Management, where users are managed." />

See [User Profiles](/docs/user-management/user-profiles) and
[User Management](/docs/user-management).

## Tools you'll see on list pages

Most list pages (Customers, Prospects, Work orders, BOLs, and more) share the
same tools:

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Search** box | Finds records by name, number, or location (depends on the page). |
| **Filters** | Opens a side panel of filters. Click the refresh icon to reset or ✕ to close. |
| **Advance Filters** | Builds a rule from **Column**, **Operator**, and **Value** (Customers, Prospects). |
| **Columns** | Chooses which columns to show. |
| **Date range** | Limits the list to a period. |
| Row menu **⋮** | Actions for that record, such as View, Edit, Export, or Delete. |
| **Jump to page / Rows per page** | Page controls at the bottom of the list. |

<Callout type="info" title="Links open in a new tab">
  Customer, project, and BOL links in lists open in a **new browser tab**. If
  nothing happens, allow pop-ups for Greenloop in your browser.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="Guaranteed destruction highlight">
  On the Work orders, BOLs, and Dispatch pages, rows **highlighted in light
  orange** are linked to **Guaranteed destruction**.
</Callout>
