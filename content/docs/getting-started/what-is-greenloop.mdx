title: What is Greenloop?
description: An operational management system for recycling industries and warehouse facilities.
---

Greenloop is an operational management system designed for **recycling
industries and warehouse facilities**. It manages the complete workflow of a
recycling operation — from customer management and pickup planning, through
warehouse processing, to final billing.


## What you can do with Greenloop

- Maintain customer information and track daily communication.
- Manage pricing, surcharges, and customer charges.
- Plan pickups and assign trucks and drivers.
- Receive, sort, and process recyclable materials.
- Record quantities and generate charges and invoices.

## How materials reach the facility

Customers can get materials to a facility in two ways:

1. **Customer-delivered** — the customer sends their own trucks.
2. **Greenloop-managed pickup** — Greenloop assigns company trucks and drivers
   to collect the materials.

Either way, every item that arrives follows a proper workflow inside the
facility — receiving, then (where configured) triage and sorting & counting.

<Callout type="info">
  Greenloop is a centralized platform. Customer records, pricing, operations,
  and billing all live in one place and stay connected.
</Callout>
