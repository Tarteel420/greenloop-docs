---
title: Creating Your First Customer
description: A quickstart for adding a commercial customer to Greenloop.
---

Every job starts with a customer. This quickstart adds a **commercial** customer
so you can move on to your first work order. For every field and the
residential form, see [Creating Customers](/docs/customer-management/creating-customers).

<UIPath items={["Customer Management", "Customers", "New customer"]} />

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Customers and click New customer
    Open the main menu, then choose **Customer Management → Customers**. Click
    **New customer** (top right) and choose **Commercial**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/new-customer-dropdown.jpg" alt="New customer dropdown" caption="① Choose Commercial or Residential." />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Fill in the primary information
    Enter the **Customer name** (required). Add the sales rep, business unit,
    lead source, and any toggles that apply.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/commercial-primary.jpg" alt="Primary information" caption="① Customer name ② Sales rep ③ Status and Lead source ④ Toggles ⑤ Copy address buttons" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter the addresses
    Fill in the **Shipping** address. Click **Copy Shipping address** to reuse it
    for **Billing** (or **Copy Billing address** the other way).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add a contact and save
    Under **Contacts**, click **Add Contact**, choose the **Contact type**, enter
    the name and phone, and click **Add**. Then click **Save** at the bottom of
    the page.

    <Screenshot src="/images/customer-management/commercial-contacts.jpg" alt="Contacts and Save" caption="① Add Contact ② Save" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check the new customer
    The customer now appears in **Customers**. Open it to manage details,
    pricing, notes, and more. → [Customer Details](/docs/customer-management/customer-details)
  </Step>
</Steps>
