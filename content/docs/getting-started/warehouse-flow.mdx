---
title: Processing Items Through the Warehouse
description: How received items move through receiving, triage, and sorting & counting, and where to follow progress on the BOL.
---

When a driver brings materials to a facility (or a customer drops them off
as a [walk-in](/docs/operations/bols/walk-in-bols)), items move through the
warehouse according to each category's **processing workflow**.

## The processing stages

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Receiving
    The **Receiver** unloads the truck, scans each item by its QR label, and
    enters it in the system. Every item starts at receiving.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Triage
    If the category requires it, bulk materials (such as Gaylord containers) go
    to **Triage** to be processed and prepared for sorting.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Sorting & Counting
    Materials are separated and categorized, for example into batteries,
    electronics, or metals.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  An item only goes as far as its category's workflow says. If no extra stage
  is set, the Receiver's entry completes the flow. Categories show their
  workflow as a badge such as **S&C** or **Triage → S&C**. See
  [Categories](/docs/catalog/categories).
</Callout>

## Follow progress on the BOL

<UIPath items={["Operations", "BOLs", "open a BOL"]} />

The BOL list has label counters for each stage:

| Column | Counters |
| --- | --- |
| **Receiving Labels** | Labels · Scanned · Pending |
| **Triage Labels** | Labels · Matched · Unmatched · Pending |
| **Sort & Count Labels** | Total · Scanned · Pending |

Open a BOL to see its processing steps: **Pickup/drop-off information →
Labels Information → Summary**. Each label shows what the Receiver, Triage, and
Sort & Count users entered.
