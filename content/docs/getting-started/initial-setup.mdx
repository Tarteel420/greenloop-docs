---
title: Initial System Setup
description: The one-time setup the Org Admin completes before operations begin.
---

When your organization first gets Greenloop, the system is **blank** except for
a single **Org Admin** account. The org admin does a one-time setup before any
operations can run.

## Setup at a glance

| Order | What | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Activate your account and change the password | Invitation email → Accesswise |
| 2 | Sign in to Greenloop | Greenloop sign-in page |
| 3 | Create users, branches, and facilities | **Access Management** (Accesswise) |
| 4 | Build the catalog | **Catalog** menu |
| 5 | Add trucks | **Operations → Fleet** |
| 6 | Configure lists, required fields, approvals, and email templates | **Configuration** menu |
| 7 | Set up pricing | **Pricing & Billing** menu |

## Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Activate your account
    Open the invitation link. Accesswise asks you to finish the required actions
    (verify your email and update your password).

    1. Click **Click here to proceed**.
    2. Enter a **New Password** and **Confirm password**, then click **Submit**.
    3. You'll see **Your account has been updated.**

    <Carousel
      images={[
        { src: "/images/getting-started/1.png", alt: "Perform the following actions", caption: "1 · Click “Click here to proceed”." },
        { src: "/images/getting-started/2.png", alt: "Update password", caption: "2 · Set and confirm your new password, then click Submit." },
        { src: "/images/getting-started/3.png", alt: "Account updated", caption: "3 · Your account has been updated." },
      ]}
    />
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Sign in
    Open the [Greenloop sign-in page](https://qa-erp.btosystems.com/auth/amplify/sign-in?returnTo=%2Fdashboard),
    enter the email the invitation was sent to, click **Continue**, then enter
    your password. The **Dashboard** opens.

    <Carousel
      images={[
        { src: "/images/getting-started/0.png", alt: "Sign in", caption: "Enter your email and click Continue." },
        { src: "/images/getting-started/4.png", alt: "Dashboard after sign-in", caption: "After signing in you land on the Dashboard. Your avatar (top right) opens the account menu." },
        { src: "/images/getting-started/5.png", alt: "Access Management", caption: "Account menu → Access Management opens Accesswise, where users are managed." },
      ]}
    />
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Create users, branches, and facilities
    Click your avatar (top right) and choose **Access Management** to open
    **Accesswise**. Invite each person, give them a Greenloop role, and assign
    their facilities. Only the **Org Admin** can create users.
    → [User Management](/docs/user-management) · [Facilities](/docs/system-resources/facilities)
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Build the Catalog
    Define the materials and items your facility handles: categories,
    subcategories, assets, packaging, and essentials.
    → [Catalog](/docs/catalog)
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Add trucks
    Add each truck with its facility, dimensions, capacity, and any
    unavailability dates. → [Fleet](/docs/system-resources/trucks)
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Configure the system
    Set up business units, lead sources, statuses, tags, lifecycle transitions,
    required fields, approval rules, and email templates.
    → [Configuration](/docs/system-resources/configuration)
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Set up pricing
    Create price tiers, surcharges, and the basic pricing template.
    → [Pricing & Billing](/docs/pricing-billing)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Only the **Org Admin** can create users. The rest of the setup (Catalog,
  System & Resources, Configuration, Pricing) can be done by the org admin or an
  Admin.
</Callout>
