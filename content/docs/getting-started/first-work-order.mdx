---
title: Creating Your First Work Order
description: A quickstart for raising a work order, which also creates its BOL.
---

A **work order** tells Greenloop what to collect, from where, and when. Saving
it schedules the driver's pickup activity and creates the paperwork (a BOL).

<Callout type="info">
  Before you start you need a [customer](/docs/getting-started/first-customer),
  a [facility](/docs/system-resources/facilities), and the relevant
  [catalog categories](/docs/catalog/categories).
</Callout>

<UIPath items={["Operations", "Work orders", "New work order"]} />

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Start a new work order
    Open **Operations → Work orders** and click **New work order**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the customer and facility
    Select the **Customers** entry. The **EPA number** and **Guaranteed
    destruction** fill in from the customer. Pick the **Facility** and the
    **WorkOrder type** (Standard or Recurring).

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-3-autofill.jpg" alt="Basic information filled from the customer" caption="① EPA number and ② Guaranteed destruction come from the customer ③ Notes" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check the addresses
    The pickup and billing addresses fill in from the customer. You can change
    them.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add pickup items
    Click **+ Pickup item**, choose a **Category** and **Sub type**, and set the
    quantity. → [Pickup Items](/docs/operations/work-orders/pickup-items)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set the estimation and route date, then save
    Check **Pallets** (use **Recalculate pallets**), pick the **Date of route**,
    optionally click **Route Suggestion**, then click **Save**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-9-estimation.jpg" alt="Estimation and save" caption="① Weight · Pallets ② Recalculate pallets ③ Date of route ④ Route Suggestion ⑤ Save" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you save, a **pickup activity** and a **BOL** are created automatically.
→ [Work Order Flow](/docs/operations/work-orders/work-order-flow)
