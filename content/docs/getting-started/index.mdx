This guide takes you from your first sign-in to a completed job. It covers what
Greenloop is, how to set up your organization, how to find your way around, and
how to finish a full cycle from customer to invoice.

## Who this is for

Greenloop is **role-based**, so each person sees only the screens and data for
their job. The guide is split by module. Read the parts that match your role
and skip the rest.

<Callout type="info">
  Not sure which role you have? See [Understanding User Roles](/docs/getting-started/user-roles).
</Callout>

## How to read this onboarding

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Learn the basics
    Read [What is Greenloop?](/docs/getting-started/what-is-greenloop) and
    [Key Concepts](/docs/getting-started/key-concepts).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Understand the roles
    [Understanding User Roles](/docs/getting-started/user-roles) explains who does what.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set up the organization
    [Initial System Setup](/docs/getting-started/initial-setup) is the one-time
    configuration the Org Admin does.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Find your way around
    [Navigating Greenloop](/docs/getting-started/navigating-greenloop) and
    [Dashboard](/docs/getting-started/dashboard) show the main menu and home screen.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Follow a job end to end
    [The Greenloop Workflow](/docs/getting-started/the-greenloop-workflow) and the
    **Quickstart** pages walk you through a real job.
  </Step>
</Steps>
