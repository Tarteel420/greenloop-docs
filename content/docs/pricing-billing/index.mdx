The Pricing & Billing module is used to create, manage, and assign **price
tiers** to customers. It also covers price adjustments, additional surcharges,
and the templates used when sharing pricing with customers.

<Screenshot src="/images/getting-started/nav-pricing-menu.jpg" alt="Pricing & Billing menu" caption="Price tiers → General / Customer specific, Price adjustments, Branch Threshold Pricing, Additional Surcharge, Templates" />

<Callout type="warn">
  All pricing is defined at the **subcategory** (sub type) level, because
  [billing is based on subcategories](/docs/catalog/subcategories).
</Callout>

## How pricing reaches a customer

1. Create a [general](/docs/pricing-billing/general-price-tiers) or
   [customer-specific](/docs/pricing-billing/customer-specific-price-tiers) price tier.
2. On the customer's page, create a
   [price tier quote](/docs/customer-management/pricing-and-quotes#create-a-price-tier-quote).
3. If required, the quote goes through [Approvals](/docs/customer-management/approvals)
   and then [customer consent](/docs/customer-management/pricing-and-quotes#require-customer-consent).
4. Use [Send pricing](/docs/customer-management/pricing-and-quotes#send-pricing)
   to email the current prices, laid out by the [Basic Template](/docs/pricing-billing/basic-template).
