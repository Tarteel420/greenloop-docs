**Additional Surcharge** manages extra charges that can be applied to customers,
for example **Fuel Surcharge** or **Service and Environmental Fee – In Metro**.

<UIPath items={["Pricing & Billing", "Additional Surcharge"]} />

<Screenshot src="/images/pricing-billing/surcharges.jpg" alt="Additional Surcharge" caption="① Warning banner ② Row menu" />

<Callout type="warn">
  The page warns: *Kindly be aware that any modifications made within this
  context will have a direct impact on every single invoice within our system.*
</Callout>

## Add a surcharge

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click More item
    Click **More item** below the list. A new row appears.

    <Screenshot src="/images/pricing-billing/surcharge-more-item.jpg" alt="More item" caption="More item" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter the name and price
    Type the **Name** and **Price** (both required).

    <Screenshot src="/images/pricing-billing/surcharge-new-row.jpg" alt="New surcharge row" caption="① Name · Price ② Save ✓ / Cancel ✕" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click the green **✓** to save, or **✕** to cancel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit or delete a surcharge

Click **⋮** on a row and choose **Edit** or **Delete**.

<Screenshot src="/images/pricing-billing/surcharge-menu.jpg" alt="Surcharge row menu" caption="Edit / Delete" />

<Callout type="info">
  Surcharges are assigned to a customer in **Additional surcharges** in the
  customer's profile information. See
  [Creating Customers](/docs/customer-management/creating-customers).
</Callout>
