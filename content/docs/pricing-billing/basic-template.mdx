The **Basic Template** defines the default structure used when sending pricing
to customers. It is loaded automatically when you click **Send pricing** on a
customer's price tier. See
[Send pricing](/docs/customer-management/pricing-and-quotes#send-pricing).

## Edit the Basic Template

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click Edit Template
    On the **Templates** page, click **Edit Template** on the **Basic
    Template** (marked **Default**).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add categories and items
    Click **+ Add Item**. Tick a whole category or individual items (use
    **Expand / Collapse All** to see them), then click **OK**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/pricing-billing/template-add-items.jpg" alt="Add items" caption="① Tick a category or items ② Expand / Collapse All ③ OK" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Arrange the order
    Drag items by the handle, or select items and use the arrows to move them to
    the top, up, down, or to the bottom. Click the bin icon to remove an item.

    <Screenshot src="/images/pricing-billing/template-edit.jpg" alt="Edit Basic Template" caption="① Add Item ② Drag ③ Move top / up / down / bottom ④ Remove ⑤ Save Template" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Save Template**, or **Cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The order you set is the order items appear whenever pricing is shared with a
customer.

## Reviewing before sending

When you send pricing, the template is shown with the customer's prices. You
can edit it for that customer (**Edit Template**, **Add Item**, **Notes**) and
**Restore to Basic Template** if needed, then click **Send**. The document
also carries the customer name and address, the expiry date, and your
organization details (**Org Details**).

<Callout type="warn">
  Sending prices is an email to the customer. Review the details before
  clicking **Send**.
</Callout>
