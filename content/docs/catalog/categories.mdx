Categories are high-level material groups, such as *Appliances* or *Batteries*.
They control how materials move through the warehouse pipeline.

<UIPath items={["Catalog", "Categories"]} />

The page has two panels: **All categories** on the left and the **Sub
categories** of the selected category on the right.

<Screenshot src="/images/catalog/categories.jpg" alt="Categories page" caption="① New category ② New sub category ③ Selected category ④ Workflow badge ⑤ Packaging requirement badge" />

Each category card shows **Operates by** (Quantity or Weight), **Type** (Pickup
or Dropoff), and a workflow badge when the category goes past receiving
(**S&C**, or **Triage → S&C**).

## Create a category

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click + in All categories
    Click the **+** button next to **All categories**. The **New category**
    window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter the details
    - **Name** (required)
    - **Operates by** (required): **Quantity** or **Weight**
    - **Type** (required): tick **Pickup**, **Drop Off**, or both

    <Screenshot src="/images/catalog/category-new.jpg" alt="New category" caption="① Name ② Operates by ③ Type ④ Workflow options ⑤ Save" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the workflow (optional)
    *Select a workflow option, or leave it default.* Choose **Goes to 'Triage'**
    and/or **Goes to 'Sorting & Counting'**. If you choose neither, the
    **Receiver** does the entry (default).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Save**. The category appears in **All categories**. Next, add its
    [subcategories](/docs/catalog/subcategories).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit or delete a category

Click **⋮** on the category card and choose **Edit** or **Delete**. Use the
**Search…** box to find a category.

## Category fields

| Field | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Category name |
| Operates by | Quantity or Weight (how items are measured) |
| Type | Pickup and/or Drop Off. In QA, the work order **Drop off items** category list shows the drop-off category *PACKAGING*. |
| Workflow | Receiver (default), Triage, Sorting & Counting |

<Callout type="info">
  If no workflow stage is chosen, items default to the **Receiver**. Otherwise
  they follow what you chose. See
  [Processing Items Through the Warehouse](/docs/getting-started/warehouse-flow).
</Callout>
