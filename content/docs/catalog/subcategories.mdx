Subcategories are the detailed items within a [category](/docs/catalog/categories),
for example *Refrigerator* and *Stove* under *Appliances*. Work orders and
labels call them **Sub types**.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Billing is always based on subcategories, not main categories.** Every price
  in a [price tier](/docs/pricing-billing) is set per subcategory.
</Callout>

<UIPath items={["Catalog", "Categories", "select a category"]} />

## Create a subcategory

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Select the category
    On the Categories page, click the category in **All categories**. Its
    subcategories load on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Click + in Sub categories
    Click the **+** button next to **Sub categories**. The **New sub category**
    window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter the details
    - **Name** (required)
    - **Doesn't require packaging**: tick it when the subcategory doesn't need packaging.
    - **Requires EPA number?**: tick it if an EPA number is needed.
    - **Packaging item(s)** (marked required): choose the packaging for this
      subcategory from the items set up in [Packaging](/docs/catalog/packaging).

    <Screenshot src="/images/catalog/subcategory-new.jpg" alt="New sub category" caption="① Name ② Doesn't require packaging ③ Requires EPA number? ④ Packaging item(s)" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Save**. The subcategory appears in the list with a **Doesn't require
    packaging** badge or its **Packaging** (for example *Packaging: 3ft-Bin*).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use **⋮** on a subcategory row for its actions.

## How packaging settings affect work orders

For subcategories that need packaging, the work order's pickup item shows
**Loose** and **Packaged** sections (for example *Alkaline Batteries* in QA).
Subcategories marked **Doesn't require packaging** only take a quantity. The
available **Item type** options depend on the category and subcategory. See [Pickup Items](/docs/operations/work-orders/pickup-items).
