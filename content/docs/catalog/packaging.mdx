Packaging defines how materials are stored and measured, for example Gaylords,
pallets, bins, and drums. There are two shapes: **Rounded** and **Rectangular**.

| Section | Columns |
| --- | --- |
| **Rounded** | Name · Diameter (In) · Capacity Calculations · Threshold |
| **Rectangular** | Name · Width (In) · Base (In) · Height (In) · Capacity Calculations · Threshold |

## Add packaging

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click New packaging
    The **New packaging item(s)** window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter the name and shape
    Enter the **Item name** (required) and choose the **Shape**: **Rectangular**
    or **Rounded**. The size fields then appear: **Width**, **Length**, and
    **Height (In)** for rectangular, or the diameter for rounded.

    <Screenshot src="/images/catalog/packaging-new.jpg" alt="New packaging item" caption="① Item name ② Shape ③ Exclude from capacity calculation" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Decide on capacity calculation
    Tick **Exclude from capacity calculation (Stacked on top)** for packaging
    that can be stacked on top of other items in the truck. A **Threshold**
    field appears.

    <Screenshot src="/images/catalog/packaging-threshold.jpg" alt="Threshold field" caption="① Shape and dimensions ② Exclude (Stacked on top) ③ Threshold ④ “Zero means always excluded from capacity calculation”" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set the threshold and save
    Enter the **Threshold** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Capacity calculation and thresholds

<Callout type="info">
  - Packaging that is **not** excluded counts toward the truck's capacity.
  - For stacked (excluded) packaging, the **Threshold** is how many items can be
    stacked. Example from the product team: with a threshold of **5**, once there
    are more than 5 items they can no longer be excluded from the capacity
    calculation.
  - A threshold of **0** means the packaging is **always excluded** from the
    capacity calculation (shown on screen).
</Callout>

The **Capacity Calculations** column shows **Yes** for packaging that counts
toward capacity and **No** for packaging excluded from it.

Use **⋮** on a row for its actions.
