**Fleet** (previously **Trucks**) manages every transport vehicle and shows what
is happening with each one: its route today, how full it is, and when it's
unavailable. [Dispatch](/docs/operations/routes) is where you plan routes;
Fleet is where you look after individual trucks. Each
[facility](/docs/system-resources/facilities) can have several trucks.

<UIPath items={["Operations", "Fleet"]} />

The page has two views: **Availability** (a weekly calendar) and **Table** (a
list). Both share the controls and summary cards at the top.

<Screenshot src="/images/system-resources/fleet-availability.jpg" alt="Fleet, Availability view" caption="① Facility ② Availability / Table ③ Week ④ Add unavailability ⑤ New truck ⑥ Summary cards ⑦ Routes on each day" />

## Facility and summary cards

Choose the **Facility** at the top. The choice is *remembered for all
Operations pages*. The cards summarize that facility for today:

| Card | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **On the road today** | Trucks with a route today, for example *1 of 1*, with the number of routes and stops. |
| **Load today** | How full today's trucks are, for example *11.2 of 100 pallets on trucks with routes*. |
| **Idle today** | Available trucks without a route today (*Every available truck has a route* when 0). |
| **Unavailable** | Trucks scheduled as unavailable this week. |
| **Route conflicts · 7 days** | Routes planned on a truck that is unavailable (*No routes on unavailable trucks* when 0). |

## Availability view

The **Availability** view shows one row per truck (name, plate, pallets, and
height) and one column per day of the week. Use **‹ ›** to change week.

- **Routes** for each day show as chips. In the demo, clicking a route took
  the user to [Dispatch](/docs/operations/routes) to plan it.
- **Unavailability** shows as a red bar across the days, with its reason or
  note.
- Hovering over an empty day shows an **Add unavailability** shortcut for that
  truck and day.

<Screenshot src="/images/system-resources/fleet-unavailability-bar.jpg" alt="Unavailability in the week" caption="① Unavailable card ② Unavailability bar" />

If a truck has a route on a day it's unavailable, it counts as a **route
conflict**. Move the route to another day, or give it to another truck or
driver in [Dispatch](/docs/operations/routes).

## Table view

<Screenshot src="/images/system-resources/fleet-table.jpg" alt="Fleet, Table view" caption="① Table ② Search ③ All · On route · Idle · Unavailable ④ Capacity today ⑤ Today's route ⑥ Driver" />

- **Search name, plate or facility** finds a truck.
- Quick filters: **All**, **On route**, **Idle**, and **Unavailable**, each with
  a count. Useful when you have many trucks.
- Columns: **Truck** (name and plate), **Facility**, **Length**, **Height**,
  **Pallets**, **Weight (lb)**, **Capacity today** (pallets free and used),
  **Today's route** (with stops done), **Driver**, **Status**, and **Routes
  (7 days)**.
- **⋮** on a row: **View**, **Edit**, and **Add unavailability**.

## Schedule truck unavailability

Mark a truck as unavailable when it's out for maintenance, repair, inspection,
and so on. While it's unavailable it isn't used for routing, and Dispatch warns
about routes on it.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click Add unavailability
    Click **Add unavailability** at the top of Fleet, on a truck's row menu, on
    from the shortcut on an empty day in the Availability view, or on the truck's page.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Fill in the details
    | Field | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Truck** * | The truck. |
    | **From** * · **To** * | The dates. *Past dates are allowed for record-keeping.* |
    | **Reason** * | **Maintenance**, **Repair**, **Inspection**, **Breakdown**, **Accident**, **Tires**, **Registration / permits**, **No driver**, **Reserved / loaned**, **Weather**, or **Other**. |
    | **Note** | Optional, for example *Brake repair*. Use it to explain **Other**. |

    <Screenshot src="/images/system-resources/fleet-add-unavailability.jpg" alt="Add unavailability" caption="① Truck ② From · To ③ Reason ④ Note ⑤ Save" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Save**. The unavailability shows in the Availability view and on
    the truck's page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Edit or remove an unavailability

Click the unavailability bar (or the **pencil** on the truck's page) to open
**Edit unavailability**. Change the details and click **Save**, or click
**Remove**. The confirmation explains what happens, for example *Removes
inspection from Oct 23, 2026 to Oct 24, 2026. Dispatch stops warning about it.*

## Truck page

Click a truck's name to open its page.

<Screenshot src="/images/system-resources/fleet-truck-page.jpg" alt="Truck page" caption="① Status ② Add unavailability · Edit · ⋮ ③ Specs ④ Upcoming routes ⑤ Unavailability" />

- The status badge, for example **Available**.
- **Specs**: facility, license plate, length, height, capacity, and weight
  capacity.
- **Upcoming routes**: date, route, driver, stops, and capacity.
- **Unavailability**: past and upcoming periods with **Reason**, **Note**, and
  **Added by**. Use the **pencil** to edit and the **bin** to remove.
- **⋮** → **Delete truck**.

## Add a truck

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click New truck
    Click **+ New truck**. The **New truck** window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### General details
    Enter the **Truck name** *, **License plate** *, and **Facility** *.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Size and capacity
    Enter **Length (ft)** *, **Height** (ft * and in), **Capacity (pallets)** *,
    and **Weight capacity (lb)** *. *Height is used to check clearance on stops.
    Capacity sets the pallet limit shown on routes.*
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Unavailability (optional)
    Click **Add a period it's unavailable** to add dates when the truck can't be
    used yet, for example while you wait for its registration.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Create
    Click **Create truck**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/system-resources/fleet-new-truck.jpg" alt="New truck" caption="① General details ② Size and capacity ③ Unavailability ④ Create truck" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Rental trucks">
  There's no separate rental option yet. To cover for a truck that's out, add
  the rental as a new truck (for example name it *Rental …*), plan routes on it,
  and give it an unavailability once it's returned. The team suggested keeping
  rental trucks in the records for history rather than deleting them, and a
  rental tag was discussed as a future addition.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Truck height and capacity feed [Route Restrictions](/docs/operations/routes/route-restrictions).
  The dashboard's **Fleet Availability for Today** widget shows how many trucks
  are available today.
</Callout>
