The **General** page holds the lists used on customer, prospect, lead, and
opportunity forms. Each list is on its own tab.

<Screenshot src="/images/configuration/general-business-units.jpg" alt="General configuration" caption="① Tabs ② Active toggle ③ Edit ④ Add (More Business Unit) ⑤ Status Filter" />

## Working with a list

Business Units, Lead Sources, and Tags all work the same way:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Add an entry
    Click the add link at the bottom of the list (**More Business Unit**,
    **More Lead Source**, or **Add Customer Tag**) and enter the name.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Edit an entry
    Click the **pencil** icon in the **Actions** column.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Activate or deactivate
    Use the **Status** toggle (**Active** / **Inactive**). Use **Status Filter**
    to show All, Active, or Inactive entries.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Business Units

Business units you can assign to customers and prospects (**Business unit**
field). They're also a filter on the Customers list.

## Lead Sources

Where a lead or customer came from, for example *Cold call*, *Customer called
in*, *Referral*, or *Website lead*. They appear in the **Lead source** field on
customers, prospects, leads, and opportunities.

## Lead Statuses

Statuses for leads. Click **New Status** to add one. Each status has a **Name**,
**Description**, **Color**, **Order**, and **Default** setting. The default
status is pre-selected on the **New lead** form. Use **⋮** to **Edit** or
**Delete** a status.

<Screenshot src="/images/configuration/general-lead-statuses.jpg" alt="Lead Statuses" caption="① New Status ② Goes cold after · Cooling period" />

### Going cold

Each status can mark leads as going cold when nobody works on them. Different
businesses move at different speeds, so you set the limit per status.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Edit the status
    Click **⋮ → Edit** on the status (or create a **New Status**).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Turn on Mark leads as going cold
    The app explains it: *Leads with no activity show as Going cold for the Warn
    before period, then as Cold once the Cold after period ends.*

    <Screenshot src="/images/configuration/lead-status-going-cold.jpg" alt="Edit Lead Status, going cold" caption="① Mark leads as going cold ② Cold after ③ Warn before" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set the periods
    - **Cold after**: how long a lead can sit in this status with no activity
      before it's **Cold**.
    - **Warn before**: how long before that the lead shows as **Going cold**.

    Choose **Hours**, **Days**, or **Weeks** for each. Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On the list, these show as **Goes cold after** and **Cooling period**. Going
cold and cold leads appear on the
[Leads Overview](/docs/lead-management/leads-overview#needs-attention) and on
the **Going cold** and **Cold** tabs of the [Leads list](/docs/lead-management/leads).

## Opportunity Statuses

The stages an opportunity moves through, each with a **Probability**:

| Status | Probability (QA example) |
| --- | --- |
| Open | 10% |
| In Discussion | 40% |
| Issued Estimates | 60% |
| Purchasing | 80% |
| Closed Won | 100% |
| Closed Lost | 0% |

Click the **edit** icon on a row to change it.

<Screenshot src="/images/configuration/general-opportunity-statuses.jpg" alt="Opportunity Statuses" caption="① Statuses and probability ② Edit" />

## Tags

Customer **Tags** are labels you set up yourself (for example *COD Customer*,
*DS Customer*, *Vendor*). Add them with **Add Customer Tag**. They appear in
the **Tags** field on the customer form, on the customer page, and in the
**Customer Tags** filter.

<Callout type="info">
  Tags are separate from a customer's lifecycle **Status**. See
  [Key Concepts](/docs/getting-started/key-concepts#the-customer-lifecycle).
</Callout>

## Custom Fields

Click **New Field** to add your own fields. The list shows **Order**, **Name**,
**Type**, **Options**, **Unique**, and **Required**, and has a **Search
fields…** box.

## Lifecycle Transitions

Controls how records move between lifecycle stages.

<Screenshot src="/images/configuration/lifecycle-transitions.jpg" alt="Lifecycle Transitions" caption="① Lead statuses allowed for promotion ② Transition permissions ③ Add Role" />

- **Lead Statuses Allowed for Lead to Prospect Promotion**: only leads in the
  selected statuses can be promoted from Lead to Prospect. Leave it empty to
  allow promotion from any lead status. Click **Add a status**.
- **Transition permissions**: choose which roles can promote records for each
  transition, and which automated action may do so on its own. Click **Add
  Role** on a row to allow another role.

| Transition |
| --- |
| Lead → Prospect |
| Prospect → Customer |
| Customer → Inactive |
| Inactive → Archived |
| Inactive/Archived → Customer (activate) |

<Callout type="warn">
  A transition marked **No roles assigned yet** can't be done by anyone until a
  role is added. On-screen note: *"An automated promotion records its own
  reason and is rejected unless it is selected here."*
</Callout>

## Pricing & Quotes

Organization-wide defaults for quotes and price tiers.

<Screenshot src="/images/configuration/pricing-quotes.jpg" alt="Pricing & Quotes" caption="① Require customer consent (organization default) ② Toggle" />

**Require customer consent**: when it's on, customer consent is required for
all quotes. This default is applied to every new customer when it's created.
Existing customers keep their own setting. See
[Pricing & Quotes for a customer](/docs/customer-management/pricing-and-quotes).
