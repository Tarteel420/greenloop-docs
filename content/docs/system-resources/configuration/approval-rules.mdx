**Approval Rules** decide who needs approval before their changes take effect.
Changes from everyone else are applied right away.

<Screenshot src="/images/configuration/approval-rules.jpg" alt="Approval Rules" caption="① Pricing rule toggle ② Roles that need approval ③ People who need approval ④ Save changes" />

## How the Pricing rule works

With **Pricing** turned on, only the roles and people listed need approval for
**price tier and project pricing changes**. The on-screen explanation:

> A price under the branch's threshold goes for approval. Anything at or above
> it is applied right away.

See also **Pricing & Billing → Branch Threshold Pricing**. That page is only
available to roles that have been given permission to it.

## Set up the rule

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Turn on Pricing
    Switch on the **Pricing** toggle.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose roles
    In **Roles that need approval**, pick one or more roles (for example
    **Sales**). This applies to anyone with the role.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose people (optional)
    In **People who need approval**, pick specific users. This applies whatever
    their role is. **They can never approve their own change.**
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Requests go to the approvers' **My inbox**. Follow them in
[Approvals](/docs/customer-management/approvals).
