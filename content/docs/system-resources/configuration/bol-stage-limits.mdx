**BOL stage limits** let an org admin decide how long a BOL can stay in each
stage before it is flagged. It works as an early-warning system: BOLs that are
taking too long are highlighted on the [BOLs](/docs/operations/bols) page so
someone can follow up. Each organization sets its own limits.

The page explains: *A BOL shows Due soon once it enters the warning window and
Over limit once it passes the limit. Time counts from the BOL's first entry into
the stage, so moving back and forth doesn't reset it. Applies to every
facility.*

## Set the limits

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open BOL stage limits
    Go to **Configuration → BOL stage limits**. The **Stage time limits** table
    lists every stage: **Waiting for pickup**, **Pickup completed**, **In
    processing**, **Ready for sign off**, and **Signed off**. Under each stage
    name, a summary shows the current setting, for example *2 hours · warn 1 hour
    before*.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Turn the limit on or off
    Use the **Limit on** toggle for each stage. Turn it off for stages you don't
    want to track, for example **Waiting for pickup** if you schedule pickups far
    ahead.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter the time limit
    In **Time limit**, enter a number and choose **hours** or **days**. A BOL
    that stays in the stage longer than this shows as **Over limit**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter the warning time
    In **Warn before**, enter how long before the limit the BOL should show as
    **Due soon**, in **hours** or **days**. Leave it empty (**None**) for no
    warning, which shows as *no warning*.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Save limits**, or **Cancel** to discard your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Invoiced** is the final stage, so it has no limit (*final stage · no limit*).

### Example

| Stage | Time limit | Warn before | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pickup completed | 9 hours | 4 hours | Receiving should start soon after the driver is back. |
| In processing | 5 days | 2 days | Processing should finish within the week. |
| Ready for sign off | 2 days | 1 day | The warehouse manager should sign off quickly. |
| Signed off | 3 days | 1 day | Leaves time before invoicing. |

*These values are the example from the sprint demo. Set your own to match your
operations.*

## Change history

Below the table, **Change history** lists every change with who made it and
when, for example *Ammar Khalid · Oct 15, 2026 12:05 am: In processing turned
on, 2 hours, warn 1 hour*.

## Where the limits show

- The **Over stage limit** card and the **Time in stage** filter (**Over
  limit**, **Due soon**, **On track**) on the [BOLs](/docs/operations/bols#time-in-stage) page.
- The **In stage** column and badges such as **Over by 7d 19h** or **Due in 3h
  57m**.
- Red-bordered cards and *… over* counts in the Board view.
- The limit line in the BOL quick view, for example *Limit for Waiting for
  pickup: 2h · warn 1h before*.
