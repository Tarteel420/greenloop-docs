## Generate an invoice

Invoices are generated through **QuickBooks**.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open a signed-off BOL
    Open the BOL and review the [Summary](/docs/operations/bols/bol-details#processing-steps).
    The BOL must be **Signed off**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Click Generate invoice
    Under **All steps completed!**, click **Generate invoice**. Greenloop sends
    the invoice request to QuickBooks and the BOL moves to **Invoiced**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/generate-invoice.jpg" alt="Generate invoice" caption="Generate invoice" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  For a guided walkthrough, see
  [Generating an Invoice](/docs/getting-started/generating-invoice).
</Callout>

## Review documents

On the BOL page, click **View BOL Documents** to open **Review documents**.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/detail-invoiced.jpg" alt="Invoiced BOL" caption="① Invoiced ② View BOL Documents ③ Processing steps" />

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/documents.jpg" alt="Review documents" caption="① BOL documents ② Export ③ Invoice status ④ Upload / Send" />

For invoiced BOLs, the **BOL documents** list can include:

- **Invoice**
- **Bill of lading (BOL)**
- **Certificate of Recycling**
- **Certificate of Destruction**

Click a document to preview it. The invoice status (for example **Invoice
approved**) shows at the top. **View linked BOL** goes back to the BOL.

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Export** a document | Click **⋮** next to it and choose **Export**. |
| **Upload document** | Click the upload (cloud) icon at the top right. |
| **Send** documents | Tick the documents (**Select documents to send**), then click the send icon (**Send selected documents**). |

<Callout type="warn">
  Sending documents emails them to the customer. Check the selection before you
  click send.
</Callout>
