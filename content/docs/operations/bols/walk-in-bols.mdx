---
title: Walk-in BOLs
description: Create a BOL for items dropped off at a facility without a work order.
---

A **walk-in BOL** records items that a customer brings to a facility (in person
or by courier) without a work order.

<UIPath items={["Operations", "BOLs", "New walk-in BOL"]} />

Click **New walk-in BOL** at the top of the [BOLs](/docs/operations/bols) page,
or use the **Create Walk-in BOL** card on the [Dashboard](/docs/getting-started/dashboard).

<Screenshot src="/images/dashboard/walkin-card.jpg" alt="Walk-in BOL card" caption="Click to start a walk-in BOL" />

## Create a walk-in BOL

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the type
    On **Create a new Walk-in BOL**, under **Customer details → Select type**,
    choose **Residential** or **Commercial**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter the customer details
    <Tabs items={["Residential", "Commercial"]}>
      <Tab value="Residential">
        Choose the **Customers** (optional) and **Facility** (required). Under
        **General details**, enter the **Name**, **Memo**, **Email**, and **Phone
        number**. Under **Address details**, enter the address (**City**,
        **State**, **Zip code**, and **Country** are required).

        <Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/walkin-residential.jpg" alt="Residential walk-in" caption="① Residential / Commercial ② Customer · Facility ③ General details ④ Address details" />
      </Tab>
      <Tab value="Commercial">
        Choose the **Customers**, **Project**, and **Facility** (required), and
        enter the **PO Number**, **EPA Number**, **Memo**, and **Guaranteed
        destruction**. Under **Drop-off type**, choose **In person** (enter the
        **Name**, required, plus Email and Phone number) or **Courier** (enter
        the **Tracking id**).

        <Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/walkin-commercial.jpg" alt="Commercial walk-in" caption="① Customer · Project · Facility · PO · EPA · Memo · Guaranteed destruction ② Drop-off type ③ Continue" />
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Continue
    Click **Continue**. The BOL is created with the status **In processing**.
    Walk-in BOLs skip the pickup stages, so in the BOL's
    [Stage stays](/docs/operations/bols#bol-quick-view) the pickup stages show
    *Not reached*.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add the labels
    In **Labels Information**, click **+ New label** for each pallet or
    container. → [Add a label](/docs/operations/bols/bol-details#add-a-label)

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/walkin-in-processing.jpg" alt="Walk-in BOL in processing" caption="① In processing ② Labels Information ③ New label" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

From here, the BOL is processed like any other. → [Managing BOL Details](/docs/operations/bols/bol-details)
