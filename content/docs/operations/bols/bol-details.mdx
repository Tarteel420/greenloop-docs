Click a BOL number to open the BOL page. It shows the job's information and its
processing steps.

## BOL Information

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** · **Date** · **Driver name** | |
| **Check in** · **Check out** · **Duration** | Recorded by the driver. |
| **Pickup address** | |
| **P.O number** · **Memo** | Click the **pencil** to edit. |
| **EPA Number** | |
| **Guaranteed destruction** | Click the **pencil** to edit. A warning shows when the BOL is associated with guaranteed destruction. |

- **View BOL Documents** opens the BOL's documents (available once there are
  documents). → [Documents & Invoicing](/docs/operations/bols/bol-documents)
- **Linked workorder** opens the work order.
- The **Notes** tab holds notes on the BOL.

## Processing steps

The **Details** tab shows three steps. Completed steps are ticked.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Pickup/drop-off information
    The **Pickup items** and **Drop off items** entered by the driver, and the
    **Total generated labels for pallets**. Click **Continue** to go to the next
    step.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/step-1-pickup.jpg" alt="Pickup/drop-off information" caption="① Pickup items ② Drop off items ③ Generated pallet labels ④ Continue" />

    While the BOL is **Waiting for pickup**, you can add items with **+ Add
    pickup item** and **+ Add drop-off item**, then click **Pickup/Dropoff done**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/detail-waiting-items.jpg" alt="Add items" caption="① Add pickup item ② Add drop-off item ③ Pickup/Dropoff done" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Labels Information
    Every pallet label, with its **Category** and **Packaging**. Expand a label
    to see its **Receiving** details (status, received by, date and time) and
    item information (**Items**, **Weight (lb)**, **Quantity**). Search by
    **label number** or filter by **Triage status**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/step-2-labels.jpg" alt="Labels Information" caption="① Search by label number · Triage status ② New label ③ Label ④ Receiving details" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Summary
    All processed items with their recorded quantity or weight. This is what
    feeds billing. Billing is based on [subcategories](/docs/catalog/subcategories).

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/step-3-summary.jpg" alt="Summary" caption="Summary" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

When every step is done, the page shows **All steps completed!** with **Signed
off by** and **Date & time**. Click **Review steps** to look back through them.
On a **Signed off** BOL, click **Generate invoice**.
→ [Documents & Invoicing](/docs/operations/bols/bol-documents#generate-an-invoice)

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/steps-completed.jpg" alt="All steps completed" caption="① Steps ② Sign-off details ③ Review steps" />

<Callout type="info">
  Label details include information entered by **Receiver**, **Triage**, and
  **Sort & Count** users, depending on the workflow configured for the
  category. → [Processing Items Through the Warehouse](/docs/getting-started/warehouse-flow)
</Callout>

## Add a label

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click New label
    In **Labels Information**, click **+ New label**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter the label details
    In **Add label**, choose the **Packaging** and **Category** (required), then
    the **Sub type** and **Quantity** (required). Click **+ More sub types** to
    add more.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/bols/add-label.jpg" alt="Add label" caption="① Packaging · Category ② Sub type · Quantity ③ Preview label ④ Save" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Preview and save
    Click **Preview label** to check it, then **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
