Drivers do not use the web system. They work through a separate **Android mobile
application**.

<Callout type="info">
  This page is based on the GreenLoop reference guide. The mobile app itself
  can't be viewed from the web system.
</Callout>

## Signing in and loading routes

When a driver signs in, the app automatically loads **all routes assigned for
that day**.

The application provides:

- Route information
- Google Maps navigation
- Pickup and drop-off details from the work order

On the web **Dispatch** page ([Classic view](/docs/operations/routes#classic-view)), the driver column shows whether the driver has been
notified and whether they have downloaded the route (for example *Never
notified*, *Not downloaded*). In the Board view, use the route's **⋮ → Driver → Notify driver**. → [Dispatch](/docs/operations/routes)

## Printing labels

Drivers use a **thermal printer** connected to the app to generate **QR code
labels**. Each label contains:

- Label Number
- Item Information
- Packaging Information
- Customer Information

The label is printed and attached to the Gaylord, bin, box, or other packaging.

## Working offline

<Callout type="info">
  The app must be **online to download routes initially**. Once routes are
  loaded, it can keep operating **without an internet connection**.
</Callout>

## Syncing back

When the route is completed, all updates made by the driver are synchronized
back to the associated [BOL](/docs/operations/bols/bol-details). You can follow
the driver's route with **Driver trail** on the [Dispatch](/docs/operations/routes)
page.
