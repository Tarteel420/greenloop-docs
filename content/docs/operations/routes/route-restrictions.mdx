---
title: Route Restrictions
description: Turn capacity and clearance height restrictions on or off for a route.
---

Restrictions help prevent assigning a truck to a stop it cannot serve. They can
be set for:

- **Capacity**
- **Clearance Height**

## Set a route's restrictions

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open the restrictions
    On the **Dispatch** page, open the route's **⋮** menu: in the
    [Board view](/docs/operations/routes#board-view) choose **Route →
    Restrictions**; in the [Classic view](/docs/operations/routes#classic-view)
    choose **Route restriction**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the restrictions
    The **Route Restrictions** window lists the *Active restrictions on route
    "…"*. Tick or untick **Capacity** and **Clearance Height**, and add a
    **Note** (for example, why a restriction is turned off).

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/restrictions.jpg" alt="Route Restrictions" caption="① Capacity · Clearance Height ② Note ③ Ok" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Ok**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How restrictions behave

> Example: if a truck has a height restriction, the system alerts you when
> adding a work order whose location cannot accommodate that truck.

<Callout type="info">
  The warning can be **bypassed** when operationally required, for example by
  parking the truck outside and moving the materials by hand.
</Callout>

Truck dimensions (height, capacity, weight capacity) are set in
[Fleet](/docs/system-resources/trucks). A stop's clearance height comes from
the work order (**Feet** / **Inches**).
