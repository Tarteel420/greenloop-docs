---
title: Route Suggestions
description: How Greenloop suggests routes based on truck availability.
---

Greenloop can **suggest routes** based on truck availability, helping you place
work orders efficiently.

## Where you see suggestions

<Tabs items={["Creating a work order", "Dispatch", "Map view"]}>
  <Tab value="Creating a work order">
    In the **Estimation** section, click **Route Suggestion** to see routes for
    the **Next 7 / 15 / 30 / 60 days**, and select one.
    → [Creating a Work Order](/docs/operations/work-orders/creating-a-work-order)

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-10-route-suggestions.jpg" alt="Suggested routes" caption="① Next 7 / 15 / 30 / 60 days ② Auto-calculated pallets" />
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Dispatch">
    On the **Dispatch** page ([Classic view](/docs/operations/routes#classic-view)), click **Route suggestion** to get
    suggestions for the selected facility and date.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Map view">
    In **Map** view, click **Suggest routes**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## When no trucks are available

If no truck can take part, Greenloop shows the message *No valid trucks are
available to take part in route suggestion*.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/suggestion-toast.jpg" alt="No valid trucks message" caption="① Route suggestion ② Message" />

<Callout type="info">
  Truck availability comes from [Fleet](/docs/system-resources/trucks), where
  you set each truck's facility, capacity, and **unavailability**.
</Callout>
