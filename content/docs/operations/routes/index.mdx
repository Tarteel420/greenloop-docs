**Dispatch** (previously **Routes**) is where dispatchers plan the locations a
driver visits during a trip. You work on **one facility at a time**.

<UIPath items={["Operations", "Dispatch"]} />

Dispatch has four views: **Board** (new), **Classic**, **Map**, and
**Calendar** (updated). **Classic** is kept for a while so dispatchers can get
used to the new screens; it will be removed later.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/dispatch-board.jpg" alt="Dispatch, Board view" caption="① Board · Classic · Map · Calendar ② Route suggestion · New route ③ Facility · Drivers · Sales people ④ Date ⑤ Fleet summary ⑥ Unplanned stops ⑦ Route card ⑧ Stops" />

## Board view

The **Board** shows one day. Pick the **Facility** (*remembered for all
Operations pages*), and optionally a driver (**All drivers**) and sales person
(**All sales people**). Use **‹ ›**, the date, or **Today** to change day.

- The **Fleet** bar shows the day's totals: trucks out (for example *1 of 1
  trucks out*), pallets used and the percentage, pallets free, drivers free, and
  how many trucks *Have room* or are *Available*.
- **Unplanned stops** on the left lists the day's work orders that aren't on a
  route yet. Search by customer or address and sort (for example **Sort: Time
  window**). Click **‹** to collapse the panel.
- Each **route card** shows the route name, the driver status (**Not
  notified**, **Notified**, or **Downloaded**), the mileage, the truck (name,
  pallets, and clearance height), the driver, and a **Capacity** bar (for example
  *11.2 / 100 pallets*).
- Each **stop** shows its order number, the customer, the location and address,
  the clearance height, the sales rep (**Rep**), the service **Window**, and the
  pallets (**Plt**). **GD** marks guaranteed destruction.

### Create a route

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click New route
    Click **+ New route** at the top, or **New route** on an empty day.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Fill in the route
    Enter the **Route name** *, check the **Date** * (the facility is shown),
    choose the **Truck** *, and optionally the **Driver**. Each truck shows its
    pallets, height, and weight, and warns if it's unavailable on a date (for
    example *Unavailable Oct 28*). You no longer need to create the route first
    and assign the driver afterwards.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/dispatch-new-route.jpg" alt="New route" caption="① Route name ② Date ③ Facility ④ Truck ⑤ Driver ⑥ Create route" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Create
    Click **Create route**. The new card shows *No stops* until you add some.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/dispatch-route-empty.jpg" alt="New empty route" caption="① Not notified ② No stops · Add stops ③ Drag stops here, or use Add to route" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add stops to a route

You can add stops in three ways:

- **Drag and drop** a stop from **Unplanned stops** onto a route card.
- Click **Add to route** on an unplanned stop and pick the route. The list shows
  each route's pallets before and after, for example *0 → 5.6 / 100 pallets*.
- Click **Add stops** on the route card, tick the stops, and click **Add stops**.
  The window has **Unplanned**, **Planned**, and **All** tabs, a search by
  customer, address, or city, sorting, and **Select all shown**. It shows the
  pallets already loaded on the truck.

<Carousel images={[
  {"src": "/images/operations/routes/dispatch-unplanned.jpg", "alt": "Unplanned stop", "caption": "① Unplanned stop ② Add to route ③ New route"},
  {"src": "/images/operations/routes/dispatch-add-to-route.jpg", "alt": "Add to route", "caption": "① Add to route ② Pick a route (pallets before → after)"},

]} />

A message confirms the change, for example *01 FOX-QA added to Docs test route
as stop 1*, and the capacity bar, mileage, and fleet totals update.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/dispatch-route-stop.jpg" alt="Route with a stop" caption="① Fleet totals ② Mileage ③ Capacity ④ Stop" />

### Stop actions

Click **⋮** on a stop:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Open work order** | Opens the stop's work order. |
| **Unplan stop** | Takes the stop off the route and back to **Unplanned stops**. |
| **Move up** · **Move down** | Changes the stop's order on the route. |

You can also drag a stop from one route card to another to move it. In the
sprint demo, the stop menu also offered moving a stop straight to another route
of the same day. In our QA check there was only one route that day, so we
couldn't confirm that option.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/dispatch-stop-menu.jpg" alt="Stop menu" caption="① Open work order · Unplan stop · Move up · Move down" />

### Route actions

Click **⋮** on a route card:

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/dispatch-route-menu.jpg" alt="Route menu" caption="① Driver actions ② Route actions ③ Export · Driver trail · Delete route" />

| Group | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| **Driver** | **Change driver**, **Clear driver**, **Notify driver…** (same as before). |
| **Route** | **Rename**, **Change date**, **Restrictions** (see [Route Restrictions](/docs/operations/routes/route-restrictions)), **Recalculate mileage**. |
| Other | **Export**, **Driver trail**, **Delete route**. |

<Callout type="warn">
  A route with a driver assigned can't be deleted. Greenloop shows *This route
  has already been assigned to a driver, unable to delete this route*. Use
  **Clear driver** first, then **Delete route**.
</Callout>

## Calendar view

The **Calendar** (previously part of Classic) shows routes by **Month** or
**Week**, with a **Needs scheduling** list on the right.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/dispatch-calendar.jpg" alt="Dispatch, Calendar view" caption="① Calendar ② Month / week navigation ③ Week / Month ④ Route legend ⑤ Needs scheduling" />

- Switch between **Week** and **Month**, move with **‹ ›**, or click **Today**.
- Each route is a coloured chip on its day, with a legend at the top. Click a
  route to open that day in the **Board** with the route highlighted, so you can
  see its stops and locations.
- Click **+** on a day to add a route for that day.

  {"src": "/images/operations/routes/dispatch-calendar-grid.jpg", "alt": "Routes on the calendar", "caption": "① Route on a day ② + on a day ③ Schedule"},
  {"src": "/images/operations/routes/dispatch-calendar-week.jpg", "alt": "Week view", "caption": "① Week ② Days ③ + on a day ④ Needs scheduling"},
]} />

### Needs scheduling

**Needs scheduling** lists work orders that still need a route, with the BOL
number, how overdue they are (for example *Overdue 5d*), and the pallets. Click
**View all** for the full list.

Click a work order to open its details in a side panel: the **pickup location**
(with **Copy address** and **Open in maps**), the contact, the route status (for
example *Not on a route · route date Oct 15, 2026*), the customer, project,
sales rep, pallets, weight, guaranteed destruction, and drop-off items. Click
**Open full page** to open the work order in a new tab.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/dispatch-wo-drawer.jpg" alt="Work order panel" caption="① Pickup location and contact ② Route · Schedule ③ Open full page" />

### Schedule a work order

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click Schedule
    Click **Schedule** next to the work order (or in its side panel). The
    **Schedule BOL #…** window shows the BOL and its pallets.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/dispatch-schedule.jpg" alt="Schedule BOL" caption="① BOL and pallets ② Route date ③ Routes on that date ④ Schedule" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the date
    Use **‹ ›**, the date, or **Today** to pick the date agreed with the
    customer. If you pick a different date from the work order's date, the
    window becomes **Reschedule BOL #…** and asks for a **Reason for
    rescheduling** *: **Rescheduled by us**, **Customer requested**, **Schedule
    correction**, **Truck or driver unavailable**, **Site not ready**,
    **Weather**, or **Other**, plus an optional **Note**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/dispatch-reschedule.jpg" alt="Reschedule reason" caption="① Reason for rescheduling ② Note ③ Route date" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the route
    The routes on that date are listed with truck, driver, stops, and the
    pallets before and after (for example *5.6 → 11.2 / 100 pallets*). Search by
    route, truck, or driver. Or choose **Create new route** and enter the
    **Route name**, **Truck**, and **Driver**.

    <Carousel images={[
      {"src": "/images/operations/routes/dispatch-schedule-routes.jpg", "alt": "Routes on the date", "caption": "① Search ② Existing route with capacity ③ Create new route"},
      {"src": "/images/operations/routes/dispatch-schedule-new-route.jpg", "alt": "Create new route while scheduling", "caption": "① Create new route ② Route name · Truck · Driver ③ Create route & reschedule"},
    ]} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Schedule** (or **Reschedule**, or **Create route & reschedule**).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  The schedule window shows each route's truck, driver, stops, and pallets, but
  not the cities of its stops. To check where a route goes, open it on the
  Board. Showing route locations and a suggested route while scheduling was
  requested in the demo as a future improvement.
</Callout>

## Classic view

The **Classic** view is the original Routes screen. It works as before.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/routes-page.jpg" alt="Classic view" caption="① Facilities ② Driver ③ Sales person ④ View ⑤ Date ⑥ Board / Classic / Map / Calendar ⑦ New route" />

### Filters and dates

Select a **Facilities** entry, and optionally a **Driver** and **Sales person**
to narrow the list. Choose the view and date:

- **View** menu: **Day**, **Work week**, **Week**, or **Month**.
- Use **‹ ›** to move between dates, or **Today** to jump back.
- Switch between **Board**, **Classic**, **Map**, and **Calendar** at the top.

Work orders associated with **Guaranteed destruction** are highlighted.

### Day view

The day view lists **Unplanned stops** (work orders not yet on a route) and the
**Routes** for the day.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/day.jpg" alt="Day view" caption="① Route row ② Driver notification / download status ③ Move to route · Remove from route · Route suggestion ④ Collapse all" />

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Name of the route |
| **Truck Name** | Assigned truck |
| **Pallets** | Total pallets |
| **Mileage (mi)** | Round trip from the facility to all stops and back |
| **Driver** | Assigned driver, with the notification status (for example *Never notified*) and whether the driver has downloaded the route (for example *Not downloaded*) |

<Callout type="info">
  Mileage is calculated as a **round trip** from the facility to all assigned
  locations and back to the facility.
</Callout>

#### Route details

Expand a route to see its work orders, with **Services** (time window),
**Customer** (name and sales rep), **Location**, and **Workorder** (**View
workorder**, **Clearance height**, **Pallets**). Click **Collapse all** to close
all routes.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/day-expanded.jpg" alt="Expanded route" caption="① Work orders on the route ② Select ③ Route actions ④ Stop actions" />

#### Move or remove stops

Tick one or more work orders, then click **Move to route** or **Remove from
route** above the list. You can also use **⋮** on a single stop.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/stop-actions.jpg" alt="Stop actions" caption="Move to route / Remove from route" />

### Route actions

Click **⋮** on a route row for:

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/route-actions.jpg" alt="Route actions" caption="Route actions" />

| Action | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **View details** | Open the route's details. |
| **Driver trail** | See the driver's trail. |
| **Assign driver** · **Clear assigned driver** | Set or remove the driver. |
| **Rename route** | Change the route name. |
| **Route restriction** | Turn capacity and clearance height restrictions on or off. → [Route Restrictions](/docs/operations/routes/route-restrictions) |
| **Export** | Export the route. |
| **Change route date** | Move the route to another date. |
| **Reorder activities** | Change the order of the stops. |
| **Recalculate Mileage** | Recalculate the round-trip mileage. |
| **Delete route** | Delete the route. |

## Map view

Click **Map** to see routes on a map. Each route card shows the truck, mileage,
driver status, and **Stops** (with time **Window**). You can:

- click **+ Add stop** to add a stop,
- drag a stop by its handle to reorder it,
- click **Suggest routes** for suggestions,
- click **Save** on the route card to keep your changes.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/routes/map.jpg" alt="Map view" caption="① Map ② Route card ③ Add stop ④ Drag to reorder ⑤ Suggest routes ⑥ Save" />
