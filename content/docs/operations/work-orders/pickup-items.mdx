title: Pickup Items
description: Define what items to collect, loose or already packaged.
---

The **Pickup items** section defines what needs to be **collected** from the
customer.

<Callout type="info">
  Categories and sub types come from the [Catalog](/docs/catalog/categories).
  The available item types depend on the category and sub type you pick.
</Callout>

## Add a pickup item

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the category
    Click **+ Pickup item** and choose a **Category**.

    <Carousel images={[
      {"src": "/images/operations/work-orders/new-7a-category.jpg", "alt": "Pickup item category", "caption": "① Category ② Sub type ③ Pickup item (add another)"},
      {"src": "/images/operations/work-orders/new-7b-category-list.jpg", "alt": "Category list", "caption": "Categories from the Catalog"},
    ]} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the sub type and quantity
    Choose the **Sub type** and set the quantity with − / +. Click **+ Sub type**
    to add another sub type to the same category, or the bin icon to remove one.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-7c-subtype.jpg" alt="Sub type and quantity" caption="① Sub type ② Quantity ③ Another sub type ④ Remove" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Say how the items are packed
    For sub types that need packaging, **Loose** and **Packaged** panels appear
    (sub types marked *Doesn't require packaging* in the
    [catalog](/docs/catalog/subcategories) don't show them). See below.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-7d-loose-packaged.jpg" alt="Loose and Packaged" caption="① Loose ② Packaged" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Loose

The customer has items but **no packaging**, so packaging must be provided.

1. In **Loose**, click **+ Packaging item**.
2. Choose the **Item type**, for example **Gaylord**, **Pallet**, or **Plastic
   Rolling Bin**. The available types depend on the sub type.
3. Enter the quantity required.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-7f-item-types.jpg" alt="Item types" caption="Item types for this sub type" />

## Packaged

The customer's items are **already stored in packaging**. In **Packaged**, click
**+ Packaging item** and choose the packaging and quantity.

Tick **Need replacement items** when replacement packaging must be provided
because the customer's packaging will be taken during pickup. Then click **+
Replacement item** to add it.

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-7e-replacement.jpg" alt="Need replacement items" caption="① Item type + quantity ② Need replacement items ③ Replacement item" />
