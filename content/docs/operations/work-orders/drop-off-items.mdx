---
title: Drop-Off Items
description: Packaging or materials delivered to the customer, and whether they are billed.
---

Drop-off items are packaging or materials that will be **delivered to the
customer**.

> Example: while collecting materials today, the customer may request additional
> Gaylords to be delivered for future use.

## Add a drop-off item

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click Drop off item
    In **Drop off items**, click **+ Drop off item**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the category and sub type
    Choose the **Category** (required, for example *PACKAGING*), then the **Sub
    type** and quantity. Click **+ Sub type** to add more.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set Bill
    Choose **Yes** or **No** in **Bill**. It defaults to **Yes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-8-dropoff.jpg" alt="Drop off items" caption="① Category ② Sub type + quantity ③ Bill" />

## The Bill field

| Bill | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **No** | Items are expected to be returned and reused for future pickups. |
| **Yes** | The customer will be charged for the items. |

<Callout type="info">
  Use **No** for reusable packaging that stays in circulation, and **Yes** when
  the customer is keeping (and paying for) the items.
</Callout>
