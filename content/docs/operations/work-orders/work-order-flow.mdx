---
title: Work Order Flow
description: What Greenloop creates automatically when a work order is saved.
---

When a work order is saved, Greenloop automatically sets up the downstream work.

## On save

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### A pickup activity is created
    A pickup activity is generated for the **driver**, who will see it in the
    [mobile app](/docs/operations/routes/driver-app).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### A BOL is generated
    A **Bill of Lading** is created automatically and tracks the job through
    pickup, receiving, and invoicing. → [BOLs](/docs/operations/bols)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Placing the work order on a route

During creation you can choose a **route suggestion**, or assign the work order
to a route afterward. → [Routes](/docs/operations/routes)

<Callout type="info">
  From here, the journey continues through the warehouse. See
  [Processing Items Through the Warehouse](/docs/getting-started/warehouse-flow).
</Callout>
