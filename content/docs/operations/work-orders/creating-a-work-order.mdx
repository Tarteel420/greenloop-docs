<UIPath items={["Operations", "Work orders", "New work order"]} />

<Callout type="info">
  Prerequisites: an existing [customer](/docs/customer-management/creating-customers),
  a [facility](/docs/system-resources/facilities), and the relevant
  [catalog categories](/docs/catalog/categories).
</Callout>

You can also start a work order from a customer's
[Work Orders tab](/docs/customer-management/customer-details#work-orders), from a
project's **Work orders → Add**, or with **Copy & Create** on an existing work
order.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click New work order
    On the **Work orders** page, click **+ New work order**. The **Create a new
    work order** page opens.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-1-basic.jpg" alt="Create a new work order" caption="① Customers ② Facility ③ WorkOrder type ④ Notes" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the customer
    Select the customer in **Customers** (required). The **EPA number**,
    **Guaranteed destruction**, and addresses fill in from the customer. The
    **Notes** icon shows a dot when the customer has notes. Click it to read them.

    <Carousel images={[
      {"src": "/images/operations/work-orders/new-2-customer.jpg", "alt": "Select the customer", "caption": "Select the customer"},
      {"src": "/images/operations/work-orders/new-3-autofill.jpg", "alt": "Filled from the customer", "caption": "① EPA number ② Guaranteed destruction ③ Customer notes"},
    ]} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Complete the basic information
    | Field | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Project** | Optional. Links the work order to a [project](/docs/customer-management/projects). |
    | **Opportunity** | Optional. |
    | **Facility** * | The facility for this job. |
    | **EPA number** | Filled from the customer. |
    | **Guaranteed destruction** | Filled from the customer, for example *Hard Drives Only*. |
    | **WorkOrder type** * | **Standard** (one-off) or **Recurring**. |

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-4-facility.jpg" alt="Choose the facility" caption="Choose the facility" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set the recurrence (Recurring only)
    If **WorkOrder type** is **Recurring**, a **Set recurrence** panel appears:

    - **Start date**
    - **Repeat every**: a number and **Year**, **Month**, **Week**, or **Day**
    - **On** (for example *September 1*) or **On the** (for example *First
      Monday of January*)
    - **End date**

    A summary below confirms the pattern, for example *every September on the 1st*.

    <Carousel images={[
      {"src": "/images/operations/work-orders/new-recurring-1.jpg", "alt": "Recurring work order", "caption": "① WorkOrder type = Recurring ② Set recurrence"},
      {"src": "/images/operations/work-orders/new-recurring-2.jpg", "alt": "Recurrence options", "caption": "① Repeat every ② On / On the ③ End date ④ Summary"},
    ]} />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check the addresses
    The **Pickup address** is filled from the customer. Choose another in
    **Pickup locations**, or click **Clear** to enter one yourself. For the
    **Billing address**, tick **Keep same as pickup address** to copy it.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-5-addresses.jpg" alt="Addresses" caption="① Pickup locations ② Clear ③ Keep same as pickup address" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add the pickup details
    Below the pickup contact, enter the **P.O no.**, the clearance height
    (**Feet** / **Inches**), the **Starting hours** and **Ending hours**, and an
    optional **Memo**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-6-pickup-details.jpg" alt="Pickup details" caption="① P.O no. · Feet / Inches ② Starting / Ending hours ③ Pickup item" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add pickup items
    Click **+ Pickup item**, choose the **Category** and **Sub type**, and set
    the quantity. → [Pickup Items](/docs/operations/work-orders/pickup-items)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add drop-off items (optional)
    Click **+ Drop off item** for packaging or materials to deliver.
    → [Drop-Off Items](/docs/operations/work-orders/drop-off-items)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check the estimation
    | Field | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Weight** | Estimated weight. |
    | **Pallets** * | Calculated automatically. You can override it; click **Recalculate pallets** to recalculate. |
    | **Date of route** * | The pickup date. |

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-9-estimation.jpg" alt="Estimation" caption="① Weight · Pallets ② Recalculate pallets ③ Date of route ④ Route Suggestion ⑤ Save" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pick a route (optional)
    Click **Route Suggestion** to see routes that fit, for the **Next 7 / 15 /
    30 / 60 days**. Select a route, or skip this and assign a route later.

    <Screenshot src="/images/operations/work-orders/new-10-route-suggestions.jpg" alt="Suggested routes" caption="① Next 7 / 15 / 30 / 60 days ② Auto-calculated pallets" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Work Order Flow](/docs/operations/work-orders/work-order-flow) for what
