The Operations module manages the complete pickup, transportation, and receiving
workflow. It is built from three connected parts.

## How the pieces fit together

<Steps>
  <Step>A [Work Order](/docs/operations/work-orders) defines the job.</Step>
  <Step>Saving it generates a [BOL](/docs/operations/bols) and a driver pickup activity.</Step>
  <Step>The work order is placed on a route in [Dispatch](/docs/operations/routes).</Step>
  <Step>The driver completes the pickup via the [mobile app](/docs/operations/routes/driver-app), syncing updates back to the BOL.</Step>
  <Step>The BOL is processed in the warehouse, signed off, and [invoiced](/docs/operations/bols/bol-documents).</Step>
</Steps>

Items a customer drops off without a work order are recorded with a
[Walk-in BOL](/docs/operations/bols/walk-in-bols).
