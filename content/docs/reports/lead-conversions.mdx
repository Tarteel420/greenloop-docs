<UIPath items={["Reports", "Lead Conversions By Sales Rep"]} />

This report shows, for each sales rep, how many leads were created and
converted.

<Screenshot src="/images/reports/lead-conversions.jpg" alt="Lead Conversions By Sales Rep" caption="① Sales rep · Lead source · Converted Between ② Expand a rep" />

## Filter the report

Narrow the report by **Sales rep**, **Lead source**, and the **Converted
Between** date range.

## Columns

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Sales Rep** | The rep (leads with no rep are grouped as *Unassigned*) |
| **Leads Created** | Leads created for the rep |
| **Converted** | Leads converted |
| **New Prospect** | Converted leads counted as new prospects |
| **Merged** | Converted leads counted as merged |
| **Cohort Rate** | Share of created leads that were converted |
| **Avg Days To Convert** | Average days from creation to conversion |

## See the leads behind the numbers

Click the arrow next to a rep to list their converted leads, with **Lead**,
**Company** (links to the record), **Source**, **Created**, **Converted**,
**Days**, **Mode** (for example *Created*), and **Promoted By**.
