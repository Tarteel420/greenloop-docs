**Lead Management** is where potential customers start. You create or import
leads, track calls and meetings with them, and **promote** them to prospects
when they're ready.

## Where leads go next

**Lead → Prospect → Customer.** Promoted leads are listed on the **Converted**
tab of the Leads list. Prospects are managed in
[Customer Management → Prospects](/docs/customer-management/prospects). Which
roles can promote, and from which lead statuses, is set in
[Lifecycle Transitions](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#lifecycle-transitions).
