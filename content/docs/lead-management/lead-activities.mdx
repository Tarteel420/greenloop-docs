Lead activities are the calls and meetings (follow-ups) scheduled with leads.
You can work with them in a **List** or a **Calendar**.

<Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/lead-activities.jpg" alt="Lead activities list" caption="① List / Calendar ② New follow-up ③ Tabs ④ Search and filters" />

## List view

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overdue** | Follow-ups still to do after their scheduled date and time. Each row shows how late it is, for example *Overdue · 58d*. |
| **Today** | Follow-ups scheduled for today. |
| **Upcoming** | Follow-ups scheduled for later. |
| **Done** | Completed follow-ups. |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled follow-ups. |
| **All** | Every follow-up. This tab also has a **Status** filter. |

- **Search subject or lead** and the **Lead**, **Assigned to**, **Type**,
  **Priority**, and **Date** filters narrow the list.
- The list shows **When** (date, time, type, priority), **Lead**,
  **Regarding**, **Description**, and **Assigned to**.
- Hover over a row and click **Complete** to mark it done, or use **⋮** for more
  actions.
- Once a filter is applied, **Save view** appears. Views work the same way as on
  the Leads list. See [Saved Views](/docs/lead-management/saved-views).

## Calendar view

Click **Calendar** at the top right.

<Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/lead-activities-calendar.jpg" alt="Calendar view" caption="① List / Calendar ② Call and Meeting toggles ③ Show completed ④ Week / Month and Today ⑤ Today and Overdue panel" />

- Switch between **Week** and **Month**, move with the **‹ ›** arrows, and click
  **Today** to come back to the current date.
- Tick **Call** and **Meeting** to show or hide each type, and **Show
  completed** to include completed follow-ups. The **Lead**, **Assigned to**,
  **Type**, and **Priority** filters also apply.
- The panel on the right lists **Today**'s follow-ups and all **Overdue** ones.
  Click one to open it (see [below](#open-a-follow-up)).

In **Month** view, click an entry on a day to open that day's follow-ups in the
list, filtered by that date.

## Open a follow-up

Click a follow-up to open it in a panel on the right.

<Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/lead-activity-drawer.jpg" alt="Follow-up panel" caption="① Status ② More actions ③ Add note ④ Complete · Reschedule · Follow up" />

The panel shows the **Lead**, **Company**, **Regarding**, **Assigned to**,
**Priority**, **Created by / on**, **Description**, and **Notes**.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Status (for example **To do**) | Change the follow-up's status. |
| **⋮** | **Edit** or **Cancel follow-up**. |
| **Add note** | Add a note to the follow-up. |
| **Complete** | Mark the follow-up as done. |
| **Reschedule** | Move it to another date or time. |
| **Follow up** | Schedule the next follow-up. |

## Create a follow-up

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click New follow-up
    On the Activities page, click **New follow-up**. You can also use
    **Schedule follow-up** on a lead.

    <Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/lead-activity-new-follow-up.jpg" alt="New follow-up" caption="① Lead ② Type ③ Date · Start time ④ Duration ⑤ Assigned to ⑥ Priority ⑦ Schedule" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the lead and type
    Search for the **Lead** by name or email. Choose **Call** or **Meeting**. For
    a meeting, also choose the **Meeting mode**: **In person** or **Online**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Set the details
    | Field | Notes |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Regarding** | Subject, up to 50 characters. |
    | **Date** * · **Start time** * | When it takes place. |
    | **Duration** * | **10 min**, **15 min**, **30 min**, **1 hr**, or **Custom**. The end time is shown below (*Ends at …*). |
    | **Assigned to** * | Defaults to you. |
    | **Priority** * | **Low**, **Medium**, or **High**. |
    | **Description** | Optional notes. |
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Schedule
    Click **Schedule**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
