Open the full lead page from the quick view (**Open full page**) or from the row
menu (**⋮ → Open full page**).

## Header

Under the lead's name you see its **status** (change it from the dropdown), the
**assignee**, the **source**, the **location**, and its tags.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Call** | Call the lead. Use the arrow to choose which number. |
| **Email** | Email the lead. |
| **Edit** | Edit the lead's details. |
| **⋮** | **Enrich profile** and **Delete lead**. |
| **Promote to prospect** | Converts the lead. See [Promoting Leads](/docs/lead-management/promoting-leads). |
| **Log activity** | Record a call or meeting that already happened (see [below](#log-an-activity)). |

## Page sections

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Next step** | The next scheduled follow-up, or **Schedule follow-up**. |
| **Contact** | **Email**, **Phone**, and **Mobile** (with copy buttons), plus **More contacts** when the lead has extra contacts. |
| **Company** | **Company**, **Website**, **Industry**, **Employees**, **Category**, and **Address**. |
| **Enrichment** | Click **Enrich** to fetch a professional profile for the lead. Once enriched, the profile is shown here and you can **Re-enrich**. |
| **About** | **Created** (date and user), **Sales repeated**, and **Description**. |
| **Notes** | Notes about the lead. Click **Add note**. |
| **Activity** | A timeline of everything that happened to the lead. Filter it by **All**, **Notes**, **Calls & meetings**, **Follow-ups**, or **Changes** (for example status changes and field updates). |

A converted lead shows a banner, *Converted to prospect on …*, with an **Open
prospect** link, and its notes and timeline sit under **Activity** and
**Lifecycle** tabs.

## Log an activity

Use **Log activity** to record a call or meeting after it happened.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click Log activity
    The **Log activity** panel opens with the **Lead** filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Fill in the details
    Choose the **Type** (**Call** or **Meeting**), add **Regarding** (up to 50
    characters), the **Date**, **Start time**, **Duration**, and **Assigned to**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the outcome
    Pick an **Outcome**: *Connected*, *No answer*, *Left voicemail*, *Meeting
    booked*, *Not interested*, *Qualified*, or *Other*.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Log call** to save it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To plan a future call or meeting instead, use **Schedule follow-up**. See
[Lead Activities](/docs/lead-management/lead-activities#create-a-follow-up).
