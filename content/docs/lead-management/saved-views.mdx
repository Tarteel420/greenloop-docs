---
title: Saved Views
description: Save your filters, tab, sort, and columns as a personal view you can reopen in one click.
---

<UIPath items={["Lead Management", "Leads", "Save view"]} />

A **view** saves the way you've set up a list so you don't have to rebuild it
every day. A view keeps the **filters**, the **quick-filter tab**, the **sort**,
and the **columns**.

Views are personal. Each user sees only the views they created. You can have
more than one view.

<Callout type="info">
  Saved views are available on the [Leads](/docs/lead-management/leads) list
  and the [lead Activities](/docs/lead-management/lead-activities) list. They
  work the same way on both.
</Callout>

## Save a view

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Set up the list
    Apply the filters you want, pick a tab, sort, and choose your columns.
    **Save view** appears at the top right once the list differs from the
    default.

    <Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-save-view-button.jpg" alt="Filters applied" caption="① Filter applied ② Filter chip ③ Save view" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Name the view
    Click **Save view**, enter a **View name**, and click **Save**.

    <Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-save-view.jpg" alt="Save view dialog" caption="① View name ② Save" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Open the view
    The view appears as a tab with a bookmark icon, after the quick-filter tabs.
    Click it any time to apply all its settings at once.

    <Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-saved-view-tab.jpg" alt="Saved view tab" caption="① Saved view tab ② View menu" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Update a view or save a copy

If you change the filters or columns while a view is open, **Update view**
appears next to **Save view**.

- Click **Update view** to save the changes into the open view.
- Or click **Save view** and choose:
  - **Save as new**: creates another view with a new name.
  - **Overwrite existing**: replaces the open view.

<Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-update-view.jpg" alt="Update view and Save view options" caption="① Update view ② Save as new / Overwrite existing" />

## Rename or delete a view

Open the view, then click **⋮** next to **Save view**:

- **Rename view**
- **Delete view**: asks you to confirm. The view is removed from your saved
  views. Your leads are not affected.

<Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-view-menu.jpg" alt="View menu" caption="① Rename view / Delete view" />
