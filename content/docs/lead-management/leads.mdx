The **Leads** list shows each **Lead** (name and email), **Company**, **Status**,
**Assignee**, **Source**, and **Next follow-up**. You can change a lead's
**Status** and **Assignee** directly from the row. In **Next follow-up**, a
**Cold** badge marks a lead that has gone cold, and **+ Schedule** adds a
follow-up.

## Quick-filter tabs

Each tab shows its count.

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All open** | Every lead that hasn't been converted to a prospect. |
| **My leads** | Open leads assigned to you. |
| **Unassigned** | Open leads with no one assigned. |
| **New this week** | Leads created this week. |
| **Going cold** | Leads close to their cold limit (the **Warn before** period). |
| **Cold** | Leads that have passed their cold limit. |
| **Converted** | Leads already promoted to prospect, with a **Promoted** date. |

When a lead goes cold is set per lead status. See
[Lead Statuses](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#lead-statuses).

## Search and filter

| Tool | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Search leads…** | Find a lead by name or email. |
| **Status**, **Assignee**, **Source**, **Tags**, **Created** | Filter the list. Pick one or more values and click **Apply**. |
| **More** | **State** (the lead's address state), **Has open follow-up**, and **Has no activities**. |
| **Date** | Filter by **Last activity**: **Today**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **This quarter**, or **Custom**. |

Applied filters appear as chips under the filter bar, for example *Assigned to:
Ahmed Hassan Khan, Ammar Khalid*. Click **×** on a chip to remove it, or **Clear
all** to remove every filter.

To keep a set of filters for daily use, save it as a view. See
[Saved Views](/docs/lead-management/saved-views).

## Choose columns

Click the **Columns** icon to show or hide columns. **Lead** is always shown.
You can add **Last activity**, **Created**, **Phone**, **Tags**, **Category**,
and **Job title**. Click **Reset to default** to go back to the standard
columns.

<Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-columns.jpg" alt="Columns menu" caption="① Columns ② Reset to default" />

## Quick view

Click a lead to open the quick view on the right, without leaving the list.
You can move from one lead to the next by clicking another row.

<Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-quick-view.jpg" alt="Lead quick view" caption="① Open full page ② Status, assignee, source, and cold badge ③ Call, Email, Edit, Promote to prospect ④ Next step" />

The quick view shows:

- The lead's **status**, **assignee**, **source**, and a **Cold** badge if it
  has gone cold.
- Actions: **Call** (choose a number from the arrow), **Email**, **Edit**, and
  **Promote to prospect**.
- **Next step**: the next scheduled follow-up, or **Schedule follow-up**.
- **Email**, **Phone**, **Mobile**, **Industry**, **Location**, **Created**, tags,
  and **Recent activity**.

Click **Open full page** to see everything about the lead. See
[Lead Details](/docs/lead-management/lead-details).

## Row menu

Hover over a row to show the **phone** icon and **⋮**. The **⋮** menu has
**View** (quick view), **Open full page**, **Edit**, **Schedule follow-up**,
**Promote to prospect**, and **Delete**.

<Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-row-menu.jpg" alt="Lead row menu" caption="① Row menu" />

## Bulk actions

Tick the box on one or more rows to show the bulk action bar: **Assign to**,
**Change status**, **Add tags**, and **Delete**. Click **Clear selection** to
untick all rows.

<Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-bulk-actions.jpg" alt="Bulk actions" caption="① Bulk action bar" />

<Callout type="info">
  Leads created or imported without an assignee land on the **Unassigned** tab.
  Use that tab, the bulk **Assign to** action, or **Assign** on the
  [Leads Overview](/docs/lead-management/leads-overview), to hand them out.
</Callout>

## Create a lead

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Click New lead
    On the Leads page, click **New lead**. The **Create a new lead** page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter the basic information
    Enter the contact's **First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, **Job title**,
    **Company**, **Website**, phone numbers, and the secondary contact and
    recipient details.

    <Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/lead-form.jpg" alt="Create a new lead" caption="① Basic information ② Lead details" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Enter the lead details
    Choose the **Status** (the default lead status is pre-selected), **Assigned
    to**, **Source**, **Industry**, **Lead category**, **No. of employees**, and
    **Tags**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add the address and extra information
    Fill in the **Address** and the **Description** under **Additional
    information**. Click **New note** to add notes. *Notes added here are saved
    once you save the lead.*
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Save
    Click **Save**. The lead appears in the Leads list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Which lead fields are required (marked *) is set in
  [Required/Optional Fields → Lead Fields](/docs/system-resources/configuration/required-optional-fields).
  Lead statuses are set in [General → Lead Statuses](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#lead-statuses).
</Callout>
