---
title: Promoting Leads
description: Move a qualified lead to the Prospect stage.
---

When a lead is qualified, promote it to **Prospect**.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Find the lead
    Open **Lead Management → Leads** and search for the lead.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose Promote to prospect
    Click **⋮** on the lead's row and choose **Promote to prospect**. You can
    also click **Promote to prospect** in the lead's quick view or at the top of
    the [full lead page](/docs/lead-management/lead-details).

    <Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-row-menu.jpg" alt="Lead row menu" caption="① Row menu with Promote to prospect" />
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Check the Converted tab
    Promoted leads are listed on the **Converted** tab with the status
    **Converted** and a **Promoted** date column.

    <Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-promoted.jpg" alt="Converted tab" caption="① Converted tab ② Converted status" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Who can promote">
  Only roles allowed in [Lifecycle Transitions](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#lifecycle-transitions)
  can promote leads. If that page limits the allowed lead statuses, only leads
  in those statuses can be promoted.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  The [Lead Conversions report](/docs/reports/lead-conversions) counts
  converted leads as **New Prospect** or **Merged**. The confirmation step after
  choosing **Promote to prospect** wasn't run while writing this guide, so its
  options aren't shown here.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Notes and activities">
  Per the sprint demo, a lead's notes and activities stay with the record when
  it is promoted, so the sales rep keeps the history. This wasn't checked in
  QA while writing this guide.
</Callout>

Next: manage the record as a [Prospect](/docs/customer-management/prospects).
Promotions per sales rep show up in the
[Lead Conversions report](/docs/reports/lead-conversions).
