The **Overview** page (titled **Team overview**) is a small dashboard for lead
data only. It shows how many leads you have, who is working on them, and which
ones need attention. The cards and links open the matching list.

<Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-overview.jpg" alt="Leads overview" caption="① Date range ② New lead ③ Summary cards" />

## Choose the date range

Use the date menu at the top right to set the period for the page. The default
is **Last 30 days**. You can choose **Today**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**,
**This quarter**, or **Custom**.

<Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-overview-date.jpg" alt="Date range options" caption="① Date range ② Range options" />

## Summary cards

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Open leads** | Leads that haven't been converted to a prospect yet. The note shows how many were active in the period, out of all open leads. |
| **New this period** | Leads created in the selected period, with the change compared with the previous period (for example *▼ 57% vs previous period*). |
| **Unassigned** | Leads with no one assigned. Click **Assign now →** to open them. |
| **Follow-ups overdue** | Follow-ups that are still to do after their scheduled date and time, with the age of the oldest one. |
| **Converted** | Leads converted to a prospect in the period, with the conversion rate. |

Click a card (or its **›** arrow) to open the matching list. For example,
**Unassigned** opens the **Unassigned** tab of the [Leads list](/docs/lead-management/leads).

## Pipeline by status and team workload

<Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-overview-pipeline.jpg" alt="Pipeline by status and Team workload" caption="① Pipeline by status ② View all leads ③ Team workload ④ Open activities" />

- **Pipeline by status** shows open leads in stage order. The bar shows the
  share of leads in each [lead status](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#lead-statuses),
  and the legend shows the count. Click **View all leads →** to open the list.
- **Team workload** shows open leads and follow-ups for each person:
  - **Leads owned**: **Open leads**, **Going cold**, and **Converted**.
  - **Follow-ups assigned**: **Overdue** and **Due today**.

  The **Unassigned** row counts leads with no owner. Click **Open activities →**
  to go to the activities list.

## Needs attention

<Screenshot src="/images/lead-management/leads-overview-attention.jpg" alt="Needs attention" caption="① Going cold ② New & untouched ③ View all" />

This section lists the leads you should act on:

- **Going cold**: leads with no recent activity, for example *No activity in 65
  days*. When a lead counts as going cold is set per lead status. See
  [Lead Statuses](/docs/system-resources/configuration/general#lead-statuses).
- **New & untouched**: new leads that nobody has contacted yet. When there are
  none, it shows *Every new lead has been contacted.*
- **Unassigned**: leads with no owner. Click **Assign** on a row to assign it.

Click **View all** next to a group to see the full list.
