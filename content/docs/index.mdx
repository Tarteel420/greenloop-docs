Welcome to **Greenloop** — the operational management platform that runs your
recycling operation end to end, from the first customer call to the final
invoice.

This documentation is written for everyone who uses Greenloop: org admins
setting the system up, sales teams managing customers, dispatchers planning
routes, and warehouse staff processing materials.
